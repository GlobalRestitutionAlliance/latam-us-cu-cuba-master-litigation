## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 30 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$295.00 Billion** in sovereign duty-free retail monopolies, expropriated international airport and cruise seaport concessions, in-flight airline catering joint ventures (**Ibeca S.A.** with European airline catering conglomerates), commercial airport handling concessions (**ECASA**), and monopolistic marine bunkering and aviation jet fuel delivery operations (**CUPET Bunkering** and **Empresa Comercializadora de Combustibles**).

Following the 1959-1960 revolutionary nationalizations, the Cuban state expropriated Havana's Rancho Boyeros Airport (now José Martí International Airport), municipal airfields across Varadero, Camagüey, Santiago de Cuba, and Holguín, the private passenger and maintenance installations of Pan American World Airways (Pan Am), historic port customs bonded warehouses, and the aviation hydrant fueling and marine bunkering storage infrastructure of American oil majors (Esso, Texaco, Sinclair). Rather than operating as non-profit public infrastructure, these assets were militarized and commercialized under the umbrella of **GAESA**, **MINTUR**, and the **Ministerio del Transporte (MITRANS)**.

Today, sovereign commercial entities extract massive, unencumbered hard-currency flows from international commerce:
1. **Tiendas Caracol S.A.** and **Aeromar S.A.** monopolize retail duty-free operations across all 10 Cuban international airports and 15 maritime cruise/marina ports, operating luxury boutiques (Habanos cigars, Havana Club rum, perfumes, electronics) in exclusive joint-venture consignment partnerships with multinational duty-free giants (**Dufry / Avolta / Swissport**).
2. **Ibeca S.A.** (In-flight Catering Joint Venture) and **ECASA** bill international scheduled and charter airlines (Air Canada, Air France, Iberia, Air Europa, Condor, Neos, Copa Airlines) hundreds of millions of dollars annually in hard currency (EUR, USD, CAD) for catering provisioning, passenger ramp handling, pushback services, baggage conveyance, and VIP salon concession fees.
3. **CUPET Bunkering** and **ECC** monopolize the provision of bonded marine bunker fuel (IFO 380, Low-Sulfur Fuel Oil, MGO) and aviation jet fuel (Jet A-1) to international container lines (MSC, Maersk, CMA CGM) and international commercial airlines, clearing transactions through offshore commodity brokers and European banking channels.
