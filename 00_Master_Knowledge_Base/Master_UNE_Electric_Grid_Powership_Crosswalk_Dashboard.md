### Strategic Mandate & Legal Foundations

Cuba's entire electrical energy generation, transmission, and distribution grid is operated as a state commercial monopoly through **Unión Eléctrica (UNE)** under the Ministry of Energy and Mines (MINEM). UNE operates as the commercial alter ego of the Cuban sovereign under *First National City Bank v. Bancec* (462 U.S. 611). In recent years, facing catastrophic domestic grid collapse, UNE entered into multi-billion-dollar commercial contracts with Turkish energy conglomerate **Karadeniz Holding** and its subsidiary **Karpower International B.V.** to deploy floating powerships moored in Havana Bay, Mariel, and Santiago de Cuba, supplying a substantial percentage of the national power supply.

The foundational physical infrastructure of UNE's national grid—including major thermal generating stations (Tallapiedra, Regla, Cienfuegos), hydroelectric dams (Hanabanilla), transmission line corridors, and substation easements—was built by and confiscated without compensation from **American & Foreign Power Company** and its operating subsidiary **Compañía Cubana de Electricidad (Cuban Electric Company - CCE)** under Law 851 (July 6, 1960). In 1969, the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission certified CCE's claim (**FCSC Claim No. CU-2578**) for **$267,568,413.62** principal—the single largest certified corporate claim in United States history. With mandatory 6% annual simple interest accrued over 66 years, the certified value of this single takings claim alone exceeds **$40.0 Billion**.

This Campaign deploys comprehensive post-judgment remedies under:
1. **First National City Bank v. Bancec (462 U.S. 611) Alter Ego Unity:** UNE is an alter ego and instrument of the Cuban state, dominated by MINCOM and the Council of Ministers, allowing judicial piercing of the corporate veil to execute against its commercial assets, electricity sales, and foreign banking clearing flows.
2. **FSIA Commercial Exceptions (28 U.S.C. §§ 1605(a)(2), 1610(a)(2)):** Commercial power generation, industrial power tariff collection in hard currency, fuel swap transactions, and floating powership charters constitute non-immune commercial activities with direct effects in international commerce.
3. **Supplemental Admiralty Rule B Maritime Bunkering Attachments:** Heavy fuel oil (HFO) tankers, bunkering barges, and fuel consignments in transit to or servicing Turkish floating powerships moored in Cuban waters are subject to maritime attachment in federal admiralty courts and international maritime jurisdictions.
4. **Helms-Burton Act Title III Private Rights of Action (22 U.S.C. § 6082):** Transnational energy corporations, equipment vendors (Siemens Energy, Wärtsilä, Shanghai Electric, Sinohydro), and fuel trading combines trafficking in confiscated CCE thermal plants, substations, and right-of-way easements are liable for statutory treble damages in U.S. federal courts.
5. **Helms-Burton Act Title IV Mandatory Sanctions (22 U.S.C. § 6091):** Mandatory visa revocations and entry exclusions for corporate officers and controlling directors of foreign energy combines trafficking in confiscated utility properties.
6. **TRIA § 201 & 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g) Garnishments:** Direct execution against commercial power clearing funds, powership charter hire floats, and intercompany receivables held in European and Turkish banks.
7. **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust (26 U.S.C. § 468B):** Channeling all energy recoveries, charter hire garnishments, and trafficking settlements into a court-supervised restitution trust for certified claimants.

### Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust Distribution Architecture

All proceeds recovered under Campaign 76—including powership charter hire garnishments under TRIA § 201, maritime fuel cargo attachments under Supplemental Admiralty Rule B, Helms-Burton Title III judgments, and electricity billing turnover orders—shall be marshaled directly into the court-supervised **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust** established pursuant to **26 U.S.C. § 468B** and **Treasury Regulation § 1.468B-1**.

The QSF Administrator shall execute distributions under a transparent, pro-rata allocation matrix:
1. **Tier 1 (Certified Pre-1959 Electrical Utility Claimants):** Successors to American & Foreign Power Company / Cuban Electric Company (CCE, FCSC Claim No. CU-2578) holding final certified awards with statutory interest.
2. **Tier 2 (Federal Terrorism & Wrongful Death Judgment Creditors):** Holders of final federal judgments against the Republic of Cuba and its instrumentalities under 28 U.S.C. § 1605A and TRIA § 201.
3. **Tier 3 (Cuban Public Restorative Energy Trust):** Allocation for modern, decentralized green energy infrastructure, renewable mini-grids, and post-authoritarian electric utility reconstruction.
