### Phased Operational Wave Analysis

#### Wave 1: Expropriated Pre-1959 Distilleries, Aging Bodegas & Proprietary Rum Brands ($80.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Complete evidentiary cross-walk to certified awards of the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) and historic industrial registries, covering the historic Cárdenas distillery and aging cellars of José Arechabala S.A. (JASA), the Bacardí Corp. San Antonio distillery in Santiago de Cuba, the Santa Cruz del Norte industrial distillation complex, regional cooperage facilities and oak aging barrel latifundia, and proprietary distillation formulas/trademarks confiscated under Law 851.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** FSIA § 1605(a)(3) commercial property expropriation exceptions, Title III Helms-Burton private right of action (22 U.S.C. § 6082), Section 211 statutory trademark bars, constructive trust tracing over modern commercial rum proceeds.

#### Wave 2: Corporación Cuba Ron S.A. Sovereign Commercial Monopolies ($70.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Direct post-judgment enforcement against state holding monopoly Corporación Cuba Ron S.A. (under MINAL), the modern industrial distilling and automated aging complex at San José de las Lajas, national rum brand commercial portfolios (Santiago de Cuba, Caney, Varadero, Legendario, Arecha), industrial alcohol synthesis monopolies, and commercial tourist concessions (Museo del Ron in Havana).
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2), (b)(2), *Bancec* alter ego corporate veil piercing, commercial receivership under Fed. R. Civ. P. 66.

#### Wave 3: Transnational Joint Ventures (Pernod Ricard / Diageo) & Distribution ($65.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Commercial garnishment and charging orders served on Havana Club Holding S.A. (Luxembourg), Havana Club International S.A., Pernod Ricard S.A. (Paris) dividend and royalty streams, Ron Santiago S.A. / Diageo plc joint venture distributions, global duty-free travel retail concessions (Dufry, Heinemann, Lagardère), and open distributor accounts receivable across Europe and Asia.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** N.Y. C.P.L.R. § 5222, French CPCE Art. L. 111-1 saisie-attribution orders, English CPR Part 72 third-party debt orders, Luxembourg Nouveau Code de Procédure Civile Art. 673 attachments.

#### Wave 4: Bulk Rum Cargo Arrests, Bonded Inventory & Trademark Actions ($50.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Service of execution orders on European customs-bonded warehouses storing physical stocks of aged Cuban rum, in rem maritime arrest of chemical parcel tankers transporting bulk rum distillates to European bottling centers, Section 211 trademark cancellation proceedings before the USPTO/TTAB, and attachment of marine transit casualty insurance proceeds.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** Supplemental Admiralty Rules B and C, Brussels I Recast Regulation (EU) No 1215/2012, Section 211 (112 Stat. 2681-88), 15 U.S.C. § 1127 note.

#### Wave 5: Commercial Alter Ego Annihilation, Receivership & Master QSF Distribution ($45.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Judicial consolidation establishing that Corporación Cuba Ron S.A., Havana Club International S.A., and MINAL are unitary alter egos of the Cuban state under *First National City Bank v. Bancec*, automated scanning and interception of U.S. correspondent bank clearing wires, appointment of an Equity Receiver, Helms-Burton Title III treble damages prosecution against spirits traffickers, and pooling of proceeds into the Master QSF.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** Fed. R. Civ. P. 53 Special Master, Fed. R. Civ. P. 66 Equity Receivership, Fed. R. Civ. P. 69 execution, 26 U.S.C. § 468B Qualified Settlement Fund administration.
