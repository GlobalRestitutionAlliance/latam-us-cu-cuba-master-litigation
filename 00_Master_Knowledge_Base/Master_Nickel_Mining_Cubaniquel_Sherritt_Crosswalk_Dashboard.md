### Executive Summary & Jurisprudential Mandate

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors **Campaign 48** of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$295.00 Billion** in sovereign industrial nickel and cobalt mining extraction combines, High-Pressure Acid Leach (HPAL) metallurgical plants, open-pit lateritic mining concessions, transnational joint ventures with foreign multinationals, ocean slurry bulk carriers, London Metal Exchange (LME) warrant warehouses, and global metal off-take contracts. Target institutions include the state nickel holding monopoly (**Grupo Empresarial del Níquel - Cubaníquel**), the state equity vehicle (**General Nickel Company S.A.**), the 50/50 commercial joint venture (**Moa Nickel S.A.**) with Canadian multinational **Sherritt International Corporation**, national processing complexes (**Pedro Sotto Alba / Moa, Che Guevara / Punta Gorda, René Ramos Latour / Nicaro**), offshore marketing conduits (**International Cobalt Company Inc. - ICCI**), the **Fort Saskatchewan refinery** in Alberta, Canada, and global commodity clearing desks.

In August 1960 under Law 851 and Executive Resolution No. 1, the Cuban regime confiscated the pioneering nickel metallurgical operations of the **Freeport Sulphur Company (operating through Moa Bay Mining Company)** in Moa, Holguín province. This taking was certified as compensable under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (FCSC Claim No. CU-2492, Decision No. CU-4920), recognizing Freeport's groundbreaking capital development of the world's first industrial-scale High-Pressure Acid Leach (HPAL) facility, deepwater ore-export wharves, and vast lateritic mineral concessions. Simultaneously, the regime seized the **Nicaro Nickel Plant**, originally built and operated with U.S. Government Defense Materials Procurement Agency financing. In 1994, the Cuban government transferred these confiscated assets into a commercial joint venture with Sherritt International Corporation, unlawfully trafficking in confiscated American property to generate billions in hard-currency nickel and cobalt export revenues.

Under **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2)** (Commercial Activity Sovereign Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(3)** (Commercial Takings Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2)**, and **28 U.S.C. § 1610(g)** under *Bancec*, Cubaníquel, General Nickel Company S.A., and Moa Nickel S.A. operate as commercial instrumentalities and alter egos of the Ministry of Energy and Mines (MINEM) and the Cuban government. Commercial joint venture dividend remittances, mixed nickel-cobalt sulfide slurry cargoes on the high seas, LME-registered Class 1 refined nickel briquettes, cobalt cathode holdings, and international off-take receivables constitute commercial property subject to maritime arrest under Supplemental Admiralty Rules B & C, Rule 69 execution, Canadian State Immunity Act commercial execution, and direct turnover into the Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF).
