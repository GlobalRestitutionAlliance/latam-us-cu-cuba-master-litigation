### Executive Summary & Jurisprudential Mandate

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors **Campaign 36** of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$275.00 Billion** in sovereign commercial insurance and reinsurance monopolies, expropriated pre-1959 American and Cuban insurance corporation reserves, state underwriting cartels (**ESICUBA** and **ESEN**), regional sanctions-bypass risk pools (**Aseguradora del Alba** and **Reaseguradora del Alba**), foreign joint-venture risk binders (hospitality, mining, energy, and container ports), outward reinsurance treaties placed in the London and European markets (Lloyd's syndicates, continental reinsurers, and broker clearing desks), and commercial aviation/maritime hull coverage escrows.

Following the revolutionary decrees of 1959 and 1960 (including Law 851, Law 890, and the nationalization of private insurance firms), the Cuban revolutionary government seized all domestic and foreign insurance institutions without paying prompt, adequate, and effective compensation. Expropriated assets included the reserves, real estate, mortgage portfolios, and premium books of premier American and Cuban insurers such as **Pan-American Life Insurance Company**, **American International Underwriters (AIU / C.V. Starr antecedents)**, **La Metropolitana**, **Compañía Cubana de Fianzas**, and **Godoy-Sayán**. In 1963, the regime established **ESICUBA** (Empresa de Seguros Internacionales de Cuba S.A.) as the exclusive state-owned monopoly licensed to underwrite international risks, joint-venture commercial investments, aviation hulls, merchant shipping, and trade credit.

Under **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2)** (Commercial Activity Sovereign Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2)** (Commercial Property Execution), and **28 U.S.C. § 1610(g)** under *Bancec*, ESICUBA, ESEN, and their offshore fronting carriers and reinsurance intermediaries operate purely commercial businesses.
