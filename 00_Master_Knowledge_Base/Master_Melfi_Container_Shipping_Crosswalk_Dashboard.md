### Strategic Mandate & Legal Foundations

Cuba's international merchant marine and maritime container shipping apparatus is operated through Panamanian front company **Melfi Marine Corporation S.A.** (incorporated offshore to evade U.S. sanctions and maritime attachment) and domestic shipping operator **Empresa de Navegación Caribe (ENC)** under the Ministry of Transport (MITRANS). Melfi Marine maintains dedicated container liner feeder routes connecting the deepwater megaport at Mariel (ZEDM / Terminal de Contenedores de Mariel) and Havana with major container hubs in Canada (Halifax, Montreal), Europe (Valencia, Barcelona, Genoa), and Mexico (Veracruz, Altamira).

To maintain global container connectivity, Melfi Marine enters into slot charter agreements and feeder connecting carrier contracts with the world's largest container shipping lines, including **Mediterranean Shipping Company (MSC)**, **CMA CGM**, and **Hapag-Lloyd**. These transnational maritime operations exploit infrastructure confiscated without compensation under Law 851, specifically the specialized railcar container ships, terminals, and deepwater piers of **Seatrain Lines Inc.** (FCSC Claim No. CU-2493), the merchant fleet of **Empresa Naviera de Cuba**, and the historic port piers of **Havana Docks Corporation** (FCSC Claim No. CU-2492).

This Campaign deploys comprehensive post-judgment remedies under:
1. **Supplemental Admiralty Rules B & C (Admiralty In Rem & Attachment Jurisdiction):** Vessels owned, managed, or beneficially operated by the Cuban state or Melfi Marine Corporation S.A., as well as commercial freights, container hire balances, and bunker fuel escrows, are subject to in rem arrest (Rule C) and maritime attachment (Rule B) in federal maritime courts and reciprocal admiralty jurisdictions worldwide (Canada, Panama, UK, Netherlands, Spain).
2. **First National City Bank v. Bancec (462 U.S. 611) Alter Ego Unity:** Melfi Marine Corporation S.A. is a sham offshore entity created and dominated by the Cuban state and MITRANS, allowing judicial piercing of the corporate veil to execute against its vessels, freights, and bank accounts.
3. **Foreign Sovereign Immunities Act (FSIA) Commercial Exceptions (28 U.S.C. §§ 1605(a)(2), 1610(a)(2)):** Commercial container shipping, time charterparty contracts, intermodal feeder services, and ocean freight collections constitute non-immune commercial activities with substantial direct effects in international commerce.
4. **Helms-Burton Act Title III Private Rights of Action (22 U.S.C. § 6082):** Transnational container mega-carriers (MSC, CMA CGM, Hapag-Lloyd) that dock at, offload cargo upon, or utilize confiscated port and container terminal properties (Havana Docks CU-2492, Mariel Blanco Rosell CU-2492) "traffic" in confiscated property and are liable for statutory treble damages in U.S. federal courts.
5. **Helms-Burton Act Title IV Mandatory Sanctions (22 U.S.C. § 6091):** Mandatory visa revocations and entry exclusions for corporate officers and directors of foreign container liner companies trafficking in confiscated Cuban maritime properties.
6. **Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA § 201) & 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g):** Attaching ocean freight remittances, container leasing per-diem clearing floats, and P&I Club Letters of Undertaking (LOUs) for deposit into the Master QSF Trust.
7. **26 U.S.C. § 468B Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust Distribution:** Directing all recovered maritime shipping proceeds into a Delaware statutory fiduciary trust for pro-rata distribution to certified maritime claimants and judgment creditors.

### Master QSF Trust Restitution & Distribution Architecture

All recoveries generated under Campaign 74 are marshaled into the **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust** established pursuant to Section 468B of the Internal Revenue Code (26 U.S.C. § 468B) and Treasury Regulation § 1.468B-1:
1. **Fiduciary Custody:** Monies obtained from Rule B maritime attachments, Rule C judicial vessel sales, charter hire garnishments, and container leasing escrow seizures are held in a Delaware statutory trust account under federal judicial oversight.
2. **Verified Claimant Allocation:** Funds are allocated strictly to certified maritime claimants (Seatrain Lines Inc. CU-2493, Havana Docks CU-2492) and holders of valid, unsatisfied federal court judgments against the Republic of Cuba and its commercial alter egos.
3. **Preclusion of Sovereign Dissipation:** Restitution proceeds are shielded from state interception, sovereign clawback, or improper redirection, ensuring transparent, court-approved victim recovery.
