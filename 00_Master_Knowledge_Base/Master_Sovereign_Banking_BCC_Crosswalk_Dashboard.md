### Strategic Mandate & Legal Foundations

Cuba's entire monetary, foreign exchange, and sovereign banking apparatus is operated as a state-dominated commercial monopoly through **Banco Central de Cuba (BCC)** and **Banco Nacional de Cuba (BNC)**, alongside military-dominated financial holding **Banco Financiero Internacional S.A. (BFI)** (under GAESA and MINFAR), and overseas banking entities including UK-incorporated **Havana International Bank Ltd. (Havin Bank London)**. Under *First National City Bank v. Bancec* (462 U.S. 611), these entities operate as commercial alter egos of the Cuban Council of Ministers and the Ministry of Foreign Trade and Investment (MINCEX).

The foundational physical assets and monetary capital underpinning this state banking monopoly—including national headquarters buildings, vault specie, branch real estate, and commercial loan portfolios—were nationalized without compensation under Law 891 (October 13, 1960):
1. **Certified Pre-1959 American Commercial Bank Claims:** Confiscation of the entire Cuban branch networks and assets of **First National City Bank of New York (Citibank)** (FCSC Claim No. CU-2623), **The Chase Manhattan Bank (JPMorgan Chase)** (FCSC Claim No. CU-2618), and **The First National Bank of Boston (Bank of America / Boston)** (FCSC Claim No. CU-2619). With 6% annual statutory simple interest accrued over 66 years, these certified takings represent tens of billions in enforceable federal liabilities.
2. **First National City Bank v. Bancec (462 U.S. 611) Offset Architecture:** The Supreme Court's seminal precedent establishing that American bank judgment creditors are entitled to pierce the corporate veil of Cuban sovereign instrumentalities and apply setoffs against sovereign debts and assets held worldwide.
3. **UK High Court Commercial Enforcement Precedents (CRF I Ltd. Decisions):** In *CRF I Ltd. v. Banco Nacional de Cuba & Republic of Cuba*, [2023] EWHC 80 (Comm), *aff'd in relevant part*, [2024] EWCA Civ 428, the English High Court of Justice and Court of Appeal established that BNC was acting in a commercial capacity (*jure gestionis*) in contracting international debt and that sovereign immunity under the UK State Immunity Act 1978 does not shield BNC or its assets from commercial judgment enforcement.
4. **Havana International Bank Ltd. (Havin Bank London) UK Asset Target:** Havin Bank Ltd. is a 100% Cuban-owned commercial bank registered in England and Wales (Company No. 01072987) and regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA). Operating from London, its equity, interbank clearing balances, and Bank of England reserve accounts are commercial assets directly subject to English Third-Party Debt Orders and charging orders under CPR Part 72 and Part 73.
5. **Terrorism Risk Insurance Act (TRIA § 201) & 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g) Garnishments:** Direct execution against blocked Cuban commercial bank accounts, correspondent clearing wires, and intercompany receivables held in U.S. and European financial institutions.
6. **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust (26 U.S.C. § 468B):** Channeling all banking recoveries, interbank wire garnishments, and commercial turnover orders into a court-supervised restitution trust for certified claimants.

### Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust Distribution Architecture

All proceeds recovered under Campaign 79—including central bank clearing floats garnished under TRIA § 201, Havin Bank London equity attachments, SWIFT interbank wire freezes, and UK High Court debt turnover orders—shall be marshaled directly into the court-supervised **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust** established pursuant to **26 U.S.C. § 468B** and **Treasury Regulation § 1.468B-1**.

The QSF Administrator shall execute distributions under a transparent, pro-rata allocation matrix:
1. **Tier 1 (Certified Pre-1959 Commercial Bank Claimants):** Successors and heirs of First National City Bank (CU-2623), Chase Manhattan (CU-2618), First National Bank of Boston (CU-2619), and other nationalized banking institutions holding final certified awards with statutory interest.
2. **Tier 2 (Federal Terrorism & Wrongful Death Judgment Creditors):** Holders of final federal judgments against the Republic of Cuba and its financial instrumentalities under 28 U.S.C. § 1605A and TRIA § 201.
3. **Tier 3 (Cuban Public Restorative Financial Trust):** Allocation for modern, independent financial system modernization, anti-money laundering compliance systems, transparent commercial banking infrastructure, and compensation for Cuban citizen savings expropriated under post-1959 monetary decrees.
