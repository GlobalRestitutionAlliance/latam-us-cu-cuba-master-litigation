## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 33 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$285.00 Billion** in sovereign commercial brewing monopolies, expropriated historic breweries and soft drink plants, multinational joint ventures (**Cervecería Bucanero S.A.** with **Anheuser-Busch InBev**, and **Los Portales S.A.** with **Nestlé S.A.**), state beverage holding combines (**Corporación Alimentaria S.A. / Coralsa** and **Empresa de Bebidas y Refrescos / EMBER**), natural mineral water concessions (**Ciego Montero**), and transnational beverage export pipelines across Canada, Europe, and Latin America.

Following the revolutionary confiscations of 1959-1960 under Law 851 and Law 890, the Cuban regime nationalized the nation's entire brewing, distilling, and non-alcoholic beverage industry without compensation. The confiscated properties included the historic and ultra-modern **Cervecería Modelo de Cotorro** (Bacardi's Hatuey brewery), the **Cervecería La Tropical** and **Cervecería Polar** complexes of the Blanco Herrera family, the nationwide bottling and distribution plants of **The Coca-Cola Bottling Company of Cuba** and **Pepsi-Cola**, and historic natural mineral water extraction springs in Guane and Ciego Montero.

In the post-Soviet era, the regime partnered with blue-chip Western multinational conglomerates to commercialize and expand these confiscated assets into multi-hundred-million-dollar hard-currency monopolies:
1. **Cervecería Bucanero S.A. (Anheuser-Busch InBev Joint Venture):** Formed in Holguín as a 50/50 commercial partnership between Coralsa and the world's largest brewer, **AB InBev**, this modern mega-brewery monopolizes the national beer market under the iconic **Cristal**, **Bucanero Fuerte**, **Mayabe**, and **Cacique** brands. The venture extracts massive hard-currency profits from foreign resort hotels, cruise passenger concessions, and hard-currency retail networks, paying millions in annual dividends through financial clearing rails in Belgium and Luxembourg.
2. **Los Portales S.A. (Nestlé S.A. Joint Venture):** Formed as a 50/50 commercial partnership between Coralsa and Swiss food & beverage giant **Nestlé S.A.** in Guane, Pinar del Río, this venture holds the exclusive national monopoly on bottled natural mineral water (**Ciego Montero**) and carbonated soft drinks (**TuKola**, **Najita**, **Mate**) supplied to all foreign-managed tourism hotels (Meliá, Iberostar, Blue Diamond) and commercial export markets.
3. **EMBER Distilleries & Regional Beverage Combines:** Monopolizing commercial domestic spirits, liqueurs (Santero, Arecha, Legendario), and industrial alcohol rectification for sovereign export.

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2) (commercial activity exception) and 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2) & § 1610(g) under *Bancec*, commercial brewing, bottling, and beverage brand distribution are purely private commercial acts (*jure gestionis*). Consequently, all multinational joint-venture dividends, export receivables, trademark licensing royalties, and foreign escrow balances are subject to immediate post-judgment garnishment and attachment in the United States, Belgium, Switzerland, Spain, Canada, and Italy.
