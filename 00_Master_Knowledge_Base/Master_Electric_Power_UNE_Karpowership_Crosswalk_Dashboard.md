### Executive Summary & Jurisprudential Mandate

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors **Campaign 45** of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$285.00 Billion** in sovereign electric power utility monopolies, base-load thermoelectric generating stations, high-voltage transmission grids, Turkish floating powership charter contracts, distributed diesel generator networks, and commercial electricity tariff collection conduits. Target institutions include the state electric holding monopoly (**Unión Eléctrica - UNE**), national base-load thermoelectric stations (**Central Termoeléctrica Antonio Guiteras, CTE Lidio Ramón Pérez / Felton, CTE Máximo Gómez / Mariel, CTE Carlos Manuel de Céspedes / Cienfuegos**), Turkish floating powership operators (**Karadeniz Holding / Karpowership**), distributed generation combine (**Empresa de Mantenimiento a Grupos Electrógenos - Geysel**), the **National Load Dispatch Center (Despacho Nacional de Carga)**, and commercial power tariff clearing accounts.

In August 1960 under Law 851 and Executive Resolution No. 1, the Cuban regime confiscated the nationwide electric utility assets of the **Cuban Electric Company (Compañía Cubana de Electricidad - CCE)**, a subsidiary of the American & Foreign Power Company (later Boise Cascade Corporation). This seizure represented the single largest corporate property claim certified by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (FCSC Claim No. CU-2578, Decision No. CU-6009), with a certified 1960 principal value of **$267,800,000.00**—now worth tens of billions with post-judgment interest and compound commercial restitution. The confiscated infrastructure included all major power generation plants, transmission substations, high-voltage lines, and distribution easements across all six historic Cuban provinces.

Under **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2)** (Commercial Activity Sovereign Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(3)** (Commercial Takings Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2)**, and **28 U.S.C. § 1610(g)** under *Bancec*, UNE operates as a commercial instrumentality and alter ego of the Ministry of Energy and Mines (MINEM) and the Cuban government. Thermal power generating stations, high-voltage transmission assets, monthly hard-currency capacity payments and charter escrows paid to Turkish floating powership operators, distributed diesel battery assets, and industrial electricity tariffs collected from foreign joint ventures constitute commercial property subject to post-judgment attachment under Supplemental Admiralty Rule B, Rule 69 execution, and direct turnover into the Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF).
