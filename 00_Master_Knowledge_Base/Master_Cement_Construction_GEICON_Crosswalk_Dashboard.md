### Executive Summary & Jurisprudential Mandate

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors **Campaign 47** of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$285.00 Billion** in sovereign cement manufacturing combines, rotary kiln clinker plants, industrial limestone and gypsum quarries, ready-mix concrete networks, specialized pneumatic bulk cement carrier vessels, and transnational joint ventures with global building material conglomerates. Target institutions include the state construction materials holding monopoly (**Grupo Empresarial de Materiales de Construcción - GEICON**), national cement manufacturing plants (**Cementos Cienfuegos S.A., Fábrica de Cemento René Arcay / Mariel, Fábrica José Mercerón / Santiago de Cuba, Fábrica Siguaney / Sancti Spíritus**), the **Punta Alegre Gypsum Quarry**, deepwater bulk clinker export terminals, and joint ventures involving European cement conglomerates (**LafargeHolcim / Holcim Ltd., Cementos Curazao**).

In August 1960 under Law 851 and Executive Resolution No. 1, the Cuban regime confiscated the premier cement manufacturing enterprise on the island: the **Lone Star Cement Corporation (operating as Compañía Cubana de Cemento Portland "El Morro")** at Mariel Bay. This taking was certified as compensable under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (FCSC Claim No. CU-0498, Decision No. CU-4980), certifying a monumental corporate property award for the confiscated state-of-the-art wet-process cement plant, deepwater export wharves, rotary kilns, bulk clinker storage silos, and vast high-calcium limestone quarries. The regime seized these facilities and transformed them into the core of its nationalized cement monopoly, trafficking in these expropriated assets to supply domestic construction, military installations, and GAESA tourist hotels.

Under **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2)** (Commercial Activity Sovereign Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(3)** (Commercial Takings Exception), **28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2)**, and **28 U.S.C. § 1610(g)** under *Bancec*, GEICON, Cementos Cienfuegos S.A., and their operating subsidiaries operate as commercial instrumentalities and alter egos of the Ministry of Construction (MICONS) and the Cuban state. Sovereign cement factories, quarry concessions, deepwater port berths, joint venture equity shares and dividend streams, specialized pneumatic bulk cement ships, and export letters of credit constitute commercial property subject to post-judgment attachment under Supplemental Admiralty Rules B & C, Rule 69 execution, and direct turnover into the Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF).
