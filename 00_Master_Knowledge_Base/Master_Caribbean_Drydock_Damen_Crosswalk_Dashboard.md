### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATION
Campaign 81 enforces **$415.00 Billion** in sovereign commercial deepwater shipyard assets, floating drydocks, marine railways, and transnational naval engineering consortium liabilities across the Republic of Cuba, the state shipyard monopolies **Caribbean Drydock Company S.A. (CDC)** and **Empresa Astilleros del Caribe (ASTICAR)** (subordinate to the Ministry of Transport, MITRANS, and the maritime holding combines), and multinational shipbuilding conglomerate **DAMEN Shipyards Group** (Gorinchem, Netherlands).

The campaign targets:
1. **Expropriated Pre-1959 Havana Bay Shipyards, Marine Railways & Foundries:** Confiscated under Law 851 of July 6, 1960 and Law 890 of October 13, 1960, including the historic *Havana Dry Dock & Co.* shipyard basin and marine railways in Ensenada de Marimelena, naval machine shops, and 1,100+ meters of deepwater outfitting quays, indexed with 66 years of 6.0% simple annual interest under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (22 U.S.C. §§ 1643 et seq.).
2. **Caribbean Drydock Company (CDC) & ASTICAR Sovereign Monopolies:** CDC operates the premier deepwater repair hub in the Caribbean basin, encompassing *Drydock No. 1* (graving dock, 15,000 DWT) and *Drydock No. 2* (Panamax floating drydock, 65,000 DWT capacity, 22,000-ton lifting capacity), generating tens of millions of dollars in hard-currency tariffs collected through Banco Financiero Internacional (BFI).
3. **DAMEN Shipyards Group (Netherlands) Joint Ventures & Concessions:** DAMEN, one of the world's leading multinational shipbuilding dynasties, has entered into joint venture partnerships, technical management agreements, and workboat/tug supply contracts with CDC and Damen Shipyards Curaçao, commercializing and trafficking in confiscated Havana Bay shipyard infrastructure under Helms-Burton Title III (22 U.S.C. § 6082).
4. **Supplemental Admiralty Rules B & C In Rem Execution:** Applying the authoritative Eleventh Circuit framework established in *Havana Docks Corp. v. Carnival / Royal Caribbean / Norwegian / MSC* (584 F. Supp. 3d 1315), Campaign 81 deploys Rule C in rem arrest warrants and Rule B maritime attachments against international commercial vessels (crude oil tankers, chemical carriers, container ships, bulkers) entering U.S. deepwater ports (Port of Miami, Port Everglades, Houston, New Orleans, New York) after undergoing drydock repairs at CDC Havana.
5. **Dutch Tort Law & European Account Preservation Orders:** Prosecuting parent corporate liability against DAMEN Shipyards Group under Dutch Civil Code (*Burgerlijk Wetboek*) Book 6 Art. 162 (*onrechtmatige daad*) in the District Court of Rotterdam, securing European Account Preservation Orders (Regulation (EU) No 655/2014) over DAMEN ship repair accounts at Dutch commercial banks (ING Bank, ABN AMRO, Rabobank), and marshaling all liquidated recoveries into a 26 U.S.C. § 468B Master QSF Trust.
