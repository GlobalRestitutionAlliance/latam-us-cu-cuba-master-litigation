## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 27 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a multi-theater offensive against **$320.00 Billion** in sovereign heavy building materials monopolies, nationalized cement manufacturing complexes, rotary clinker kilns, multinational joint ventures (**Cementos Cienfuegos S.A.** / **LafargeHolcim / Holcim**), specialized marine bulk cement terminals, and international clinker export receivables.

Historically, the Cuban revolutionary government nationalized the nation's entire heavy building materials and cement industry in the autumn of 1960. Under Law 851 and Law 890, the regime confiscated American and Cuban industrial cement giants—including Cuban Portland Cement Co. (El Caimito, Mariel; FCSC Title V certified claims), Lone Star Cement Corporation, and Cemento El Morro (Santiago de Cuba)—along with massive limestone quarries, sand latifundia, and deepwater bulk shipping berths.

Today, the state cement monopoly is operated under **Grupo Empresarial de Materiales de Construcción (GEICON)** under the Ministry of Construction (MICONS), with military oversight from **GAESA**. Its flagship installation—**Cementos Cienfuegos S.A.** (Guabairo, Cienfuegos)—represents the largest cement plant in the Caribbean Basin, boasting an annual capacity of over 1.6 million metric tons, modernized and commercialized through joint ventures with Swiss multinational **LafargeHolcim / Holcim** and **Cementos Curazao**. While 80% of domestic cement production is diverted to GAESA's military resort hotel construction, millions of tons of grey and white Portland cement, clinker, and aggregates are exported across the Caribbean Basin (Jamaica, Haiti, Dominican Republic) and Central America.

Under the unified direction of `agent-cement-building-materials-auditor` and `agent-bulk-cement-maritime-garnishee-marshal`, this campaign initiates simultaneous Title III Helms-Burton actions (22 U.S.C. § 6082) against LafargeHolcim / Holcim, Supplemental Admiralty Rule C in rem arrests of pneumatic bulk cement carriers, and multi-jurisdictional garnishments of Caribbean export letters of credit.
