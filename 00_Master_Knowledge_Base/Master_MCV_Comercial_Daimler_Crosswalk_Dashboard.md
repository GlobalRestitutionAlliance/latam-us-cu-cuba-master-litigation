### EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ARCHITECTURAL FOUNDATION
Campaign 82 enforces **$420.00 Billion** in sovereign commercial vehicle dealerships, assembly plants, heavy equipment distribution concessions, and transnational automotive conglomerate liabilities across the Republic of Cuba, the state monopoly **Empresa Mixta MCV Comercial S.A.** (a 50/50 joint venture between Daimler and the military-commercial conglomerate **Corporación CIMEX S.A. / GAESA** under the Ministry of Transport, MITRANS), German automotive parent conglomerates **Daimler Truck AG** (Leinfelden-Echterdingen) and **Mercedes-Benz Group AG** (Stuttgart), and foreign commercial fleet suppliers.

The campaign targets:
1. **Expropriated Pre-1959 Dealerships, Assembly Lines & Distribution Networks:** Confiscated under Law 851 of July 6, 1960 and Law 890 of October 13, 1960, including the extensive Cuban branch, parts depots, and assembly lines of *General Motors Corporation* (FCSC Claim Nos. CU-3087 / CU-3088), the historic *Ambar Motors S.A.* flagship headquarters and master showroom (Vedado, Havana, 23 y Infanta), *Ford Motor Company S.A. / CREDESCO* (FCSC Claim No. CU-3072, Decision No. CU-4015), and private Cuban automotive import houses (*Compañía Importadora de Autos y Camiones S.A.*), indexed with 66 years of 6.0% simple annual interest under Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (22 U.S.C. §§ 1643 et seq.).
2. **Empresa Mixta MCV Comercial S.A. Sovereign Monopolies:** MCV Comercial, operating its central automotive and assembly complex at Vía Blanca y Vía Monumental (Berroa, Havana) and nationwide branches across 8 provinces, controls the exclusive distribution, assembly, maintenance, and fleet leasing of Mercedes-Benz commercial trucks (Actros, Atego, Axor, Arocs), Setra/Mercedes-Benz passenger coaches (Viazul, Transtur, Transgaviota), Daimler Fuso trucks, and MTU Friedrichshafen / Rolls-Royce Power Systems industrial powerplants.
3. **Helms-Burton Title III (22 U.S.C. § 6082) & Title IV Trafficking Actions:** Prosecuting parent corporate liability against Daimler Truck AG and Mercedes-Benz Group AG for knowingly and intentionally trafficking in confiscated Cuban automotive properties, distribution rights, and dealership concessions, demanding statutory treble damages and submitting executive visa revocation dossiers under Title IV (22 U.S.C. § 6091) against corporate directors.
4. **German Supply Chain Due Diligence Act (LkSG §§ 3, 7) Regulatory Enforcement:** Initiating administrative enforcement petitions before the German Federal Office for Economic Affairs and Export Control (BAFA) targeting Daimler's reliance on state-mandated employment agencies (ASETUR / CUBATECNICA) that confiscate 90–98% of Cuban mechanics' and assembly workers' earned wages, establishing actionable forced-labor supply chain liability under German and EU law (CSDDD).
5. **German ZPO Attachments & Master QSF Restitution:** Deploying pre-judgment attachment petitions under German ZPO §§ 916 et seq. in the Landgericht Stuttgart and Frankfurt to freeze automaker corporate dividend distributions and commercial accounts, serving European Account Preservation Orders (Regulation (EU) No 655/2014) on German clearing banks (Deutsche Bank, Commerzbank, LBBW), issuing English CPR Part 72 third-party debt orders on Havin Bank London, and channeling all liquidated recoveries into a 26 U.S.C. § 468B Master QSF Trust.
