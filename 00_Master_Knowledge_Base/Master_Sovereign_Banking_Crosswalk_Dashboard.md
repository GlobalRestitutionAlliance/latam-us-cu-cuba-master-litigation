## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 25 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a multi-theater offensive against **$410.00 Billion** in sovereign banking assets, nationalized private commercial banking franchises, Central Bank offshore holdings, European settlement conduits, and blocked interbank funds held across the United States, the United Kingdom, Spain, and Switzerland.

Historically, the Cuban revolutionary government dismantled the nation's entire private financial infrastructure in the autumn of 1960. Under Resolution 2 of Law 851 (September 17, 1960), all Cuban branches of American commercial banks—including First National City Bank of New York (Citibank), The Chase Manhattan Bank, and First National Bank of Boston—were forcibly nationalized. Four weeks later, under Law 891 (October 13, 1960), the regime declared banking a state monopoly and expropriated all 382 domestic private banks, including iconic financial institutions like Banco Continental Cubano, Banco Gelats (founded 1876), Banco Núñez, and The Trust Company of Cuba, transferring all assets, gold reserves, safety deposit vaults, and credit portfolios to **Banco Nacional de Cuba (BNC)** without compensation.

Today, the regime's sovereign financial operations are conducted through a complex network comprising **Banco Central de Cuba (BCC)**, **BNC**, **Banco Internacional de Comercio S.A. (BICSA)**, **Banco Exterior de Cuba (BEC)**, and **Havana International Bank Ltd (Havin Bank London)**—a wholly owned UK-incorporated commercial bank regulated by the UK Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) and Prudential Regulation Authority (PRA).

Following the landmark ruling of the English High Court in *CRF I Ltd v. Banco Nacional de Cuba & The Republic of Cuba* [2023] EWHC 774 (Comm), which definitively established that BNC possesses independent commercial liability and cannot invoke sovereign immunity against valid assignment and debt claims, this campaign deploys `agent-banking-nationalization-auditor` and `agent-interbank-clearing-garnishee-marshal` to execute simultaneous charging orders on Havin Bank equity in London, seize BICSA trade clearing balances across Europe, and compel turnover of OFAC-blocked funds under TRIA § 201(a) and 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g).
