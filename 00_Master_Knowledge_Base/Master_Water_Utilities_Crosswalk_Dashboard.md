## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 29 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a multi-theater offensive against **$280.00 Billion** in sovereign water and wastewater utility monopolies, expropriated historic municipal aqueducts (**Acueducto de Albear**, private American-owned waterworks), multinational joint ventures (**Aguas de La Habana, S.A.** with **Agbar / Suez / Veolia**), commercial resort utility billing concessions (**Aguas de Varadero**), hydroelectric power generation plants (**Central Hidroeléctrica Hanabanilla**), and industrial sea-water reverse-osmosis desalination complexes in offshore tourism keys.

Historically, the Cuban revolutionary government expropriated the nation's entire municipal water supply and sanitation infrastructure under Law 851 and Law 890 in 1960. The confiscations encompassed the historic Acueducto de Albear (the renowned 19th-century civil engineering landmark supplying Havana's metropolitan core), American-owned private water supply companies, watershed basins, deep subterranean extraction wells, and agricultural irrigation networks, transferring all water assets to the **Instituto Nacional de Recursos Hidráulicos (INRH)** without compensation.

In 2000, the regime commercialized these confiscated public utilities by forming **Aguas de La Habana, S.A.**, a 50/50 joint venture with Spanish multinational **Sociedad General de Aguas de Barcelona (Agbar)**, now integrated into French multinational **Veolia / Suez**. For over 25 years, Agbar and Veolia have operated the commercial water concession for Havana, extracting millions of euros annually in technical management fees, engineering contracts, and dividends while deploying expropriated colonial and pre-revolutionary water mains. Concurrently, **Aguas de Varadero** monopolizes commercial water and sanitation services for over 50 foreign-managed luxury resort hotels (Meliá, Iberostar, Blue Diamond), billing commercial utility tariffs exclusively in hard currency (EUR/USD).

Under the unified leadership of `agent-water-utility-concessions-auditor` and `agent-utility-tariff-garnishee-marshal`, this campaign initiates simultaneous Title III Helms-Burton actions (22 U.S.C. § 6082) against Agbar and Veolia, *Bancec* veil-piercing under 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g), third-party debt orders on foreign hotel utility payments, and precautionary attachments on European management accounts in Madrid and Paris.
