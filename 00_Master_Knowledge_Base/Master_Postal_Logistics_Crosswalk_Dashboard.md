## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 31 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$275.00 Billion** in sovereign postal monopolies, expropriated historic post office real estate and air mail routes, transnational express courier joint ventures (**DHL Express Cuba**), sovereign air cargo and maritime parcel consolidation combines (**Aerovaradero S.A.** and **Transcargo S.A.**), Universal Postal Union (**UPU**) terminal dues clearing rails in Switzerland, and offshore ecommerce package delivery pipelines (**Supermarket23, MallHabana, Katapulk, EnvíosCuba**).

Following the revolutionary confiscations of 1959-1960 under Law 851 and Law 890, the Cuban regime nationalized the nation's entire postal, telecommunications, and freight transport infrastructure without compensation. Confiscated properties included the historic Central Post Office of Havana (**Palacio de Correos de Cuba**), prime metropolitan real estate, regional postal dispatch centers, and the private air mail concession facilities operated by Pan American World Airways and American freight forwarders.

Today, rather than functioning as a non-commercial public service, the state's postal and freight apparatus has been converted into a massive hard-currency extraction engine controlled by **Grupo Empresarial Correos de Cuba (GECC) OSDE**, **Empresa de Mensajería y Cambio Internacional (EMCI)**, **Aerovaradero S.A.** (MITRANS / ECASA subsidiary), and the **GAESA** military logistics apparatus:
1. **Commercial Parcel Clearance & Customs Surcharges:** The regime extracts tens of millions of dollars annually by levying mandatory hard-currency customs processing charges, handling fees, and delivery surcharges on millions of humanitarian, food, medicine, and consumer goods parcels sent by the Cuban diaspora.
2. **International Postal Clearing Rails:** Under Universal Postal Union (UPU) treaties, Correos de Cuba maintains active bilateral mail exchange and terminal dues clearing accounts with foreign postal administrations (Correos de España, Canada Post, USPS, La Poste), with multilateral balances cleared through UPU financial rails in Berne, Switzerland.
3. **Air Freight & Courier Concessions:** Aerovaradero S.A. operates dedicated cargo handling terminals at Havana's José Martí International Airport and regional airports, billing foreign air carriers (Iberia Cargo, Copa Cargo, Air Canada Cargo, Aeroméxico Cargo) for ramp freight handling, pallet de-containerization, and cold storage in hard currency (EUR/USD).
4. **Transnational Ecommerce Portals:** Offshore merchant entities (Supermarket23, MallHabana, Katapulk, EnvíosCuba) sell groceries, appliances, and remittances-in-kind to overseas families with credit card charges processed through acquiring bank rails in Spain, Panama, and Canada, while physical deliveries are fulfilled locally through Correos de Cuba and Transcargo distribution networks.

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2) (commercial activity exception) and 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2) & § 1610(g) under *Bancec*, all commercial air freight receivables, UPU terminal dues settlement balances, offshore ecommerce merchant payouts, and courier agency remittances are subject to immediate post-judgment garnishment and execution across the United States, Switzerland, Spain, Panama, Canada, and the United Kingdom.
