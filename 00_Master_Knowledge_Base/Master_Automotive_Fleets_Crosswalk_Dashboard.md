## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Architecture

This Evidentiary Cross-Walk Dashboard anchors Campaign 32 of the Global Restitution Alliance post-judgment enforcement apparatus. It operationalizes a coordinated multi-theater offensive against **$265.00 Billion** in sovereign automotive distribution monopolies, tourist vehicle rental combines (**Transtur S.A.** with brands **REX, Havanautos, Cubacar**), military passenger transportation fleets (**Transgaviota** under GAESA), multinational automotive joint ventures (**MCV Comercial S.A.** with **Daimler AG / Mercedes-Benz**), nationwide automotive repair and parts retail networks (**SASA**), and heavy construction/agricultural machinery import combines (**Transimport, Construimport**).

Following the revolutionary confiscations of 1959-1960 under Law 851 and Law 890, the Cuban regime seized the entire commercial automotive ecosystem of the island without compensation. Confiscated properties included the prime showrooms, assembly hangars, parts depots, and service facilities of American automakers (General Motors, Ford, Chrysler, Mack Trucks, International Harvester), as well as private metropolitan bus companies (**Omnibus Aliados de La Habana**, **Autobuses Modernos S.A.**), provincial motorcoach lines, and independent taxi fleets.

Rather than providing non-profit public transportation, the Cuban state has built an aggressive commercial monopoly designed to capture foreign currency from international tourists and business operations:
1. **Commercial Tourist Vehicle Rentals:** **Transtur S.A.** (MINTUR) operates thousands of modern imported vehicles under the REX (luxury), Havanautos, and Cubacar brands, leasing them to foreign tourists at rates among the highest in the Caribbean. Rentals are billed exclusively in foreign hard currency (EUR, USD, CAD), with reservations pre-paid through European Global Distribution Systems (**Amadeus**, **Sabre**), online travel agencies (OTAs), and acquiring banks in Spain and Panama.
2. **GAESA Military Tourism Fleets:** **Transgaviota** operates a nationwide fleet of modern luxury motorcoaches (Yutong, Marcopolo) and executive vans providing chartered airport transfers, interprovincial excursions, and hotel shuttles for all foreign-managed resort complexes, generating steady hard-currency flows paid directly by international tour operators (TUI, Sunwing, Transat).
3. **Multinational Commercial Automotive Joint Ventures:** **MCV Comercial S.A.** operates as a long-standing commercial joint venture between the Cuban state and German multinational **Daimler AG / Mercedes-Benz**, holding the exclusive commercial monopoly on the import, assembly, servicing, and distribution of Mercedes-Benz passenger cars, vans, heavy trucks, and buses across Cuba.
4. **Automotive Parts & Service Monopolies:** **SASA (Servicios Automotores S.A.)** and CIMEX automotive divisions extract hard currency through monopolistic retail sales of imported automotive parts, lubricants, batteries, and mandatory maintenance services to joint ventures, embassies, and private transport operators.

Under 28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2) (commercial activity exception) and 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2) & § 1610(g) under *Bancec*, tourist vehicle rentals, commercial automotive assembly, and fleet leasing constitute commercial activities (*jure gestionis*). Consequently, all foreign prepaid rental vouchers, tour operator transport balances, automotive joint-venture profits, and commercial dealership escrows are subject to immediate post-judgment garnishment and attachment across the United States, Germany, Spain, Canada, Panama, and Switzerland.
