### Phased Operational Wave Analysis

#### Wave 1: Expropriated Pre-1959 Urban Latifundia, Commercial Office Buildings & Mansions ($80.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Complete evidentiary cross-walk to certified awards of the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) and historic municipal property registers, covering prime confiscated commercial acreage along 5ta Avenida in Miramar, iconic Vedado commercial high-rises and former corporate headquarters, Old Havana colonial commercial warehouses and portside real estate confiscated under Law 851, and luxury residential estates in Cubanacán converted into hard-currency commercial rentals for foreign diplomatic missions.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** FSIA § 1605(a)(3) commercial property expropriation exceptions, Title III Helms-Burton private right of action (22 U.S.C. § 6082), constructive trust tracing over modern commercial rental proceeds.

#### Wave 2: Inmobiliaria Monte Barreto S.A. & Miramar Trade Center Commercial Complex ($70.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Direct post-judgment enforcement against Inmobiliaria Monte Barreto S.A. and the six commercial office towers of the Miramar Trade Center complex (Edificios Habana, Santiago, Santa Clara, Matanzas, Cienfuegos, Camagüey), encompassing over 100,000 sq meters of premium commercial office space, automated parking structures, retail concourses, and telecommunications riser infrastructure.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2), (b)(2), *Bancec* alter ego corporate veil piercing, N.Y. C.P.L.R. § 5222 restraining notices served on multinational corporate tenant headquarters, and commercial receivership under Fed. R. Civ. P. 66.

#### Wave 3: GAESA Inmobiliaria Almest & Habaguanex Commercial Properties ($65.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Commercial disgorgement of Inmobiliaria Almest S.A. (GAESA's direct military real estate monopoly), Habaguanex S.A. commercial buildings and restored boutique real estate in Old Havana, long-term hard-currency lease contracts with foreign diplomatic chanceries, United Nations agencies (UNDP, UNESCO, UNICEF, WHO/PAHO), and commercial bonded logistics warehousing around Havana.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** 28 U.S.C. § 1610(a)(2), commercial exceptions to diplomatic immunity under Vienna Convention Art. 31(1)(a), Third-Party Garnishment under Fed. R. Civ. P. 69.

#### Wave 4: Offshore Lease Settlement Escrows, Transnational Real Estate JVs & Title III Trafficking Actions ($50.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Interdiction of offshore collection accounts in Spain, Panama, Switzerland, and Andorra utilized by sovereign landlords, execution upon European-Cuban real estate joint venture equity (Real Inmobiliaria S.A.), filing of Title III Helms-Burton treble damages claims against corporate tenants knowingly occupying confiscated real estate, and interception of international correspondent wire lease settlements.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** Title III statutory treble damages (22 U.S.C. § 6082), foreign debt enforcement under Spanish LEC Art. 584 and Swiss SchKG Art. 271, electronic wire transfer interception under N.Y. U.C.C. § 4-A-503.

#### Wave 5: Commercial Alter Ego Annihilation, Receivership & Master QSF Distribution ($45.00B Pool)
- **Scope:** Judicial unification establishing that Inmobiliaria Monte Barreto, Almest, Habaguanex, and GAESA are unitary alter egos of the Cuban state under *First National City Bank v. Bancec*, appointment of an Equity Receiver under Fed. R. Civ. P. 66 to assume landlord rights and collect rent, worldwide CPLR § 5222 restraining notices served on European corporate parent boards, foreclosure writs encumbering sovereign real property overseas, and pooling of proceeds into the Master QSF.
- **Enforcement Mechanisms:** Fed. R. Civ. P. 66 Equity Receivership, Fed. R. Civ. P. 53 Special Master, 26 U.S.C. § 468B Qualified Settlement Fund administration.
