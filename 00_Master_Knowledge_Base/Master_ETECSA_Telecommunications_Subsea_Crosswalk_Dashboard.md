### Strategic Mandate & Legal Foundations

Cuba's state-owned telecommunications apparatus is operated through sole sovereign commercial monopoly **Empresa de Telecomunicaciones de Cuba S.A. (ETECSA)**, its cellular subsidiary **Cubacel**, and broadband provider **Nauta**, controlled by the Ministry of Communications (MINCOM). ETECSA operates as the commercial alter ego of the Cuban sovereign under *First National City Bank v. Bancec* (462 U.S. 611), generating billions of dollars in hard currency annually from foreign telecommunications services, roaming clearinghouses, and direct settlement agreements with major U.S. carriers.

The entire physical infrastructure, switching backbone, and terrestrial network of ETECSA was founded upon the uncompensated expropriation under Law 851 (July 6, 1960) of the **Cuban Telephone Company**, a subsidiary of the American multinational **International Telephone & Telegraph Corporation (ITT)**. ITT's certified claim before the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (**FCSC Claim No. CU-2615**) was certified in 1969 for **$130,657,868.00** principal—the second largest certified corporate claim in U.S. history. With mandatory 6% annual simple interest accrued over 66 years, the current uncompensated balance exceeds **$20.0 Billion**.

This Campaign deploys comprehensive post-judgment remedies under:
1. **TRIA § 201 & FSIA § 1610(a)(2)/(g) Garnishments (*Alejandre v. Republic of Cuba*):** Establishing that direct telecommunications carrier interconnect settlement payments payable to ETECSA by authorized U.S. carriers (AT&T, Verizon, T-Mobile, IDT / Boss Revolution) licensed under OFAC 31 C.F.R. § 515.542 are attachable non-immune commercial assets subject to turnover in satisfaction of federal judgments.
2. **First National City Bank v. Bancec (462 U.S. 611) Alter Ego Unity:** ETECSA is an alter ego and instrument of the Cuban state, dominated by MINCOM, allowing judicial piercing of the corporate veil to execute against its commercial assets, spectrum dues, and foreign banking clearing flows.
3. **Subsea Fiber-Optic Cable In Rem & Maritime Attachment:** Dark fiber indefeasible rights of use (IRU) and landing concessions traversing international waters and landing at Cojímar (ALBA-1 consortium with Venezuela/Jamaica and ARCOS-1 system) constitute attachable commercial property under Supplemental Admiralty Rule B.
4. **Helms-Burton Act Title III Private Rights of Action (22 U.S.C. § 6082):** Transnational telecommunications carriers, equipment manufacturers, and systems vendors trafficking in confiscated ITT / Cuban Telephone Company switching centers, central offices, and spectrum rights are liable for statutory treble damages in U.S. federal courts.
5. **Helms-Burton Act Title IV Mandatory Sanctions (22 U.S.C. § 6091):** Mandatory visa revocations and entry exclusions for corporate officers and directors of foreign telecommunications entities trafficking in confiscated communications property.
6. **Transnational Digital Top-Up Remittance Interdiction:** Garnishments and charging orders directed against foreign exchange settlement floats held by global airtime aggregators (Ding / Ezetop, Recharge.com, DTOne, Fonoma) capturing remittances destined for ETECSA and Transfermóvil.
7. **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust (26 U.S.C. § 468B):** Consolidating all telecommunications recoveries, interconnect garnishments, and trafficking settlements into a court-supervised restitution trust for certified claimants.

### Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust Distribution Architecture

All proceeds recovered under Campaign 75—including carrier interconnect garnishments under TRIA § 201, international roaming clearinghouse attachments, Helms-Burton Title III judgments, and digital top-up processor levies—shall be marshaled directly into the court-supervised **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust** established pursuant to **26 U.S.C. § 468B** and **Treasury Regulation § 1.468B-1**. 

The QSF Administrator shall execute distributions under a transparent, pro-rata allocation matrix:
1. **Tier 1 (Certified Pre-1959 Telecommunications Claimants):** Successors to ITT / Cuban Telephone Company (FCSC CU-2615), RCA Communications (CU-0071), and Western Union (CU-0824) holding final certified awards with statutory interest.
2. **Tier 2 (Federal Terrorism & Wrongful Death Judgment Creditors):** Holders of final federal judgments against the Republic of Cuba and its instrumentalities under 28 U.S.C. § 1605A and TRIA § 201 (*Alejandre*, *Weininger*, *Villoldo*).
3. **Tier 3 (Cuban Civil Society & Digital Rights Restitution):** Allocation for independent telecommunications infrastructure, unrestricted digital connectivity funds, and human rights restitution programs.
