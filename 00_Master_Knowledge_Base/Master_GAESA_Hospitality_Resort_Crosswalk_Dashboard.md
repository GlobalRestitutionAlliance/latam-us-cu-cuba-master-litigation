### Strategic Mandate & Legal Foundations

Cuba's commercial tourism hospitality sector is dominated by the military-business holding company **Grupo de Administración Empresarial S.A. (GAESA)** through its specialized hospitality arm, **Grupo de Turismo Gaviota S.A.**, alongside state commercial enterprises **Gran Caribe S.A.** and **Cubanacán S.A.** Under *First National City Bank v. Bancec* (462 U.S. 611), GAESA and Gaviota operate as the commercial alter egos of the Cuban Ministry of the Revolutionary Armed Forces (MINFAR) and the Council of Ministers, capturing billions of dollars in foreign exchange through hotel room revenues, food and beverage operations, yacht charters, and international tour bookings.

The underlying physical assets supporting this luxury empire—including prime beachfront real estate, coastal keys, and historic Havana hotels—were seized without compensation from American and Cuban owners under Law 851 (July 6, 1960) and Law 890 (October 14, 1960):
1. **The Sanchez-Hill Certified Latifundia Claim (FCSC Claim No. CU-2495):** The uncompensated confiscation of Santa Lucía Company S.A. / Central Santa Lucía encompassing 100,000+ acres of coastal land in Holguín, including the prime beachfronts of Playa Esmeralda where Meliá Hotels International and Gaviota erected the luxury resorts *Paradisus Río de Oro* and *Sol Río de Luna y Mares*.
2. **Expropriated Landmark Luxury Hotels:** Nationalization of the Hotel Habana Riviera (FCSC Claim No. CU-0249, constructed in 1957 by Meyer Lansky and associates at Paseo del Prado / Malecón), the historic Hotel Nacional de Cuba, and the Hotel Habana Hilton (Habana Libre).
3. **FSIA Commercial Exceptions (28 U.S.C. §§ 1605(a)(2), 1610(a)(2)):** Commercial resort operations, hotel management contracts, marina berthing, and tour operator credit agreements constitute non-immune commercial activities with direct effects in the United States and international commerce.
4. **Helms-Burton Act Title III Private Rights of Action (22 U.S.C. § 6082):** Transnational hotel management corporations (Meliá, Iberostar, Blue Diamond), online travel agencies (Expedia, Booking.com, Trivago, Hotelbeds), and European tour operators trafficking in confiscated beachfront properties and urban hotels are liable for statutory treble damages (*Del Valle v. Trivago*; *Herederos de Roberto Gomez Cabrera v. Meliá / Gaviota*).
5. **Helms-Burton Act Title IV Executive Sanctions (22 U.S.C. § 6091):** Mandatory visa revocations and entry exclusions for corporate officers and controlling directors of foreign hotel combines trafficking in confiscated resort properties (demonstrated by the U.S. State Department's Title IV sanctioning of Meliá CEO Gabriel Escarrer Jaume).
6. **Supplemental Admiralty Rule B Marina & Vessel Attachments:** Catamarans, excursion yachts, and marine bunkering supplies servicing Gaviota's Marina Gaviota Varadero and Marina Marlin networks are subject to maritime attachment in federal admiralty courts.
7. **TRIA § 201 & 28 U.S.C. § 1610(g) Garnishments:** Direct execution against hotel booking settlement funds, GDS transaction fees, and tour operator credit balances held in European, Canadian, and U.S. banks.
8. **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust (26 U.S.C. § 468B):** Channeling all hospitality recoveries, booking garnishments, and trafficking judgments into a court-supervised restitution trust for certified claimants.

### Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust Distribution Architecture

All proceeds recovered under Campaign 78—including hotel management fees garnished under TRIA § 201, GDS transaction receivables attached under Rule 69, online travel agency settlements, and Helms-Burton Title III judgments—shall be marshaled directly into the court-supervised **Master Qualified Settlement Fund (QSF) Trust** established pursuant to **26 U.S.C. § 468B** and **Treasury Regulation § 1.468B-1**.

The QSF Administrator shall execute distributions under a transparent, pro-rata allocation matrix:
1. **Tier 1 (Certified Pre-1959 Coastal & Hotel Claimants):** Successors and heirs of the Sanchez-Hill family (CU-2495), Meyer Lansky interests (CU-0249), and other certified beachfront and hotel proprietors holding final certified awards with statutory interest.
2. **Tier 2 (Federal Terrorism & Wrongful Death Judgment Creditors):** Holders of final federal judgments against the Republic of Cuba and its military-commercial instrumentalities under 28 U.S.C. § 1605A and TRIA § 201.
3. **Tier 3 (Cuban Public Restorative Environmental & Coastal Trust):** Allocation for ecological remediation of damaged coral reefs, sustainable beach preservation, independent coastal property restitution, and local community infrastructure development.
