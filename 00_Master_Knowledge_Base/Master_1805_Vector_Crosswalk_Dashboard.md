## 3. WAVE 1 HARDENING REPORT: THE VANGUARD BEACHHEAD (VECTORS 0001–0250)

### Primary Verified Archival Anchors
1. **Vector 0001 — The 1854 Ostend Manifesto:**
   - *Archive:* NARA RG 59 (General Records of the Department of State), Diplomatic Despatches.
   - *Legal Nexus:* Premeditated mens rea to acquire Cuba by purchase or force, establishing historical non-consensual annexation intent.
   - *FRE Rule:* FRE 803(16) (Ancient Documents), FRE 803(8) (Public Records).
2. **Vector 0002 — The 1859 Slidell Thirty Million Dollar Bill (S. 497):**
   - *Archive:* U.S. Capitol Archival Collections / NARA RG 46 & 233.
   - *Legal Nexus:* Direct legislative attempt to appropriate federal funds to secure Cuban latifundia, proving early commercial expropriation motives.
3. **Vectors 0056–0070 — Military Civil Orders Nos. 62, 89, 139 (1899–1902):**
   - *Archive:* NARA RG 140 (Records of the Military Government of Cuba).
   - *Legal Nexus:* Unilateral executive decrees by Major General Leonard Wood partitioning communal sugar haciendas, extinguishing customary Cuban land titles, and converting them to corporate fee simple.
   - *Procedural Hook:* Creates the fiduciary trust standard under *United States v. Mitchell*, 463 U.S. 206 (1983) and *United States v. White Mountain Apache Tribe*, 537 U.S. 465 (2003).
4. **Vector 0113 — Major General Leonard Wood Dispatch to Senator Orville H. Platt (Dec 6, 1900):**
   - *Archive:* NARA RG 94 (Adjutant General's Office Entry 297, File 351402).
   - *Legal Nexus:* Direct written confession that the U.S. Military Government deliberately subverted the Cuban Constituent Assembly to preserve sugar trust monopolies.
5. **Vector 0208 — Major General Leonard Wood Dispatch to President William McKinley (Oct 29, 1901):**
   - *Archive:* Library of Congress Manuscript Division, Leonard Wood Papers, Container 31.
   - *Legal Nexus:* Official executive admission that the Platt Amendment left Cuba with little or no independence, designed to maintain U.S. control of the global sugar tariff.
