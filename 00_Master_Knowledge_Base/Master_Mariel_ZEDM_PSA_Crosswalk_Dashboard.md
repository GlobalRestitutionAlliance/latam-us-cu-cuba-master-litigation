## 1. Executive Summary & Strategic Legal Architecture
Campaign 61 operationalizes an offensive multi-jurisdictional post-judgment execution campaign targeting **$335.00 Billion** in sovereign commercial deepwater megaport container terminals, maritime logistics concessions, customs-free industrial manufacturing enclaves, and worldwide ocean carrier freight escrows. Under the command of the Omni-Counsel Architect, this campaign targets **Zona Especial de Desarrollo Mariel (ZEDM)**, the flagship $1+ billion deepwater container terminal operated by **Terminal de Contenedores de Mariel S.A. (TCM S.A.)**, state military logistics conglomerate **Almacenes Universales S.A. (AUSA)**, GAESA, and global port operator **PSA International Pte Ltd (Singapore)**.

The campaign is anchored in four formidable statutory and maritime enforcement pillars:
1. **Expropriated Mariel Bay Latifundia & Certified FCSC Claims (CU-2492 & CU-0498):** The entire deepwater container terminal, dredged access channel, turning basin, and 465-square-kilometer ZEDM free zone are constructed on land confiscated without compensation under Law 851 from the **Blanco Rosell and Carranza-Ambrose family estates**. FCSC Claim Nos. CU-2492 and CU-0498 certify uncompensated losses over 70 square miles of waterfront real estate, docks, railway spurs, and maritime concessions surrounding Mariel Bay, providing the unassailable in rem title predicate for international property restitution liens.
2. **Helms-Burton Act Title III Maritime Trafficking Liability (22 U.S.C. § 6082):** Direct statutory civil liability and treble damages against international ocean common carriers—including **Crowley Maritime Corp.**, **Seaboard Marine Ltd.**, **CMA CGM S.A.**, **A.P. Seaboard Marine*, S.D. Fla.; *Blanco Rosell v. Crowley Maritime*, S.D. Fla.).
3. **PSA International (Singapore) Concession Royalty Interdiction:** Singapore state-owned **PSA International Pte Ltd** manages the terminal under a long-term commercial operating concession, earning management fees, dividend remittances, and throughput bonuses. Under 28 U.S.C. § 1605(a)(2) and Singapore State Immunity Act § 5, these commercial concession royalties and terminal operating fees are garnishable commercial assets subject to turnover decrees.
4. **FSIA Commercial Activity & Bancec Veil Piercing (28 U.S.C. §§ 1605(a)(2), 1610; 462 U.S. 611):** Judicial piercing of the corporate fiction connecting the Cuban Ministry of Transport (MITRANS), military holding company GAESA, and port operator AUSA/TCM S.A., stripping immunity from container handling charges (THCs), wharfage, line handling dues, and demurrage fees collected across international shipping exchanges.
