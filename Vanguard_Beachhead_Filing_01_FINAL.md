## III. SPECIFIC SEEMINGLY NARROW REQUESTS FOR MANDATORY JUDICIAL NOTICE
Pursuant to Fed. R. Evid. 201(b)(2), (c)(2), and (d), Petitioners formally supply the necessary information and demand that this Court take mandatory judicial notice of the following discrete, official adjudicative facts:
- **Adjudicative Fact No. 1 (NARA RG 140 — Civil Orders 62, 89, 139): ** Between January 1, 1899, and May 20, 1902, the United States Military Government of Cuba, exercising plenary governing authority under Major General Leonard Wood, promulgated executive decrees—including Civil Order No. 62 (March 5, 1902), Civil Order No. 89 (May 27, 1901), and Civil Order No. 139 (April 25, 1900)—which restructured Cuban mortgage redemption proceedings, mandated the demarcation and partition of communal estates (Haciendas Comuneras), and authorized public foreclosure auctions of encumbered agricultural parcels at distressed valuations (NARA RG 140).
- **Adjudicative Fact No. 2 (NARA RG 234 — FCSC Certified Claims Ledgers): ** Between 1965 and 1972, the United States Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC), acting pursuant to Title V of the International Claims Settlement Act of 1949 (Public Law 88-666), officially adjudicated and certified 5,911 property claims against the Government of Cuba, determining a total principal certified value of $1,851,057,358.00 bearing 6% simple annual interest, and compiling master cadastral and title ledgers deposited in NARA Record Group 234.
- **Adjudicative Fact No. 3 (NARA RG 94 — Wood Dispatch of December 6, 1900): ** On December 6, 1900, Military Governor Major General Leonard Wood transmitted an official dispatch to United States Senator Orville H. Platt (Chairman of the Senate Committee on Relations with Cuba) expressing dissatisfaction with the elected delegates to the Cuban Constituent Assembly and proposing federal legislative conditions that subsequently formed the text of the Platt Amendment (NARA RG 94, Entry 297, File 351402).
- **Adjudicative Fact No. 4 (LOC Manuscript Division — Wood Dispatch of October 29, 1901): ** On October 29, 1901, Military Governor Major General Leonard Wood officially reported to President William McKinley that under the Platt Amendment, 'there is, of course, little or no independence left in Cuba,' that 'the island is practically an American dependency,' and that the United States would 'practically control the sugar trade of the world' (Library of Congress Manuscript Division, Papers of Leonard Wood, Container 31).
