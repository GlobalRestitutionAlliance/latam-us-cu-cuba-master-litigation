## Consensus & Branch Protection Requirements
- **Protected Branch:** `main` is permanently protected. Direct pushes are disabled.
- **Mandatory Reviews:** Pull requests require a minimum of 2 approving reviews from designated CODEOWNERS.
- **Cryptographic Enforcement:** Every commit must have an immutable, verified GPG signature.
- **Automated CI Barrier:** The `integrity-verification` pipeline must pass with 100% hash parity before merging.
