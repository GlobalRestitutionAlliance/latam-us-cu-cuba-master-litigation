### I. ADMISSIBILITY OF FCSC CERTIFIED VALUATIONS

#### A. Conclusive Party-Opponent Admissions Under FRE 801(d)(2)(D)

The Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) is an official agency of the United States. Its formal certified claims ledgers (NARA RG 234) constitute binding admissions of a party-opponent regarding property descriptions, ownership, and valuations.

#### B. Preclusion of Government Contradiction

The Government is judicially and equitably estopped from disputing valuations that its own quasi-judicial commission certified under federal statute.

### II. SUN TZU ENTRAPMENT & RED TEAM REBUTTAL

#### Red Team Rebuttal: 

FRE 803(8) public records hearsay exception completely establishes admissibility.
