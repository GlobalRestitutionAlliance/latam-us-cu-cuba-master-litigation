### I. JUDICIAL FINDINGS OF FACT & CONCLUSIONS OF LAW

#### A. Likelihood of Success and Irreparable Harm

The Court finds that Plaintiffs have demonstrated a substantial likelihood of success on their claims under the Tucker Act and Mitchell doctrine, and that irreparable harm would result from the dissipation of blocked accounts.

#### B. Balance of Equities

The balance of equities strongly favors preserving the res of the constructive trust.

### II. ORDER OF RESTRAINING & MANDATORY DIRECTIVES

#### 1. Restraining OFAC and Treasury

Defendants are hereby enjoined from transferring, expending, or licensing any blocked Cuban accounts under 31 C.F.R. Part 515.

#### 2. Mandatory Accounting within 30 Days

Defendants shall file a verified accounting of all funds and accrued interest held in blocked accounts since July 8, 1963.

#### 3. Bond Waiver

The bond requirement under Fed. R. Civ. P. 65(c) is hereby waived in the interest of justice.
