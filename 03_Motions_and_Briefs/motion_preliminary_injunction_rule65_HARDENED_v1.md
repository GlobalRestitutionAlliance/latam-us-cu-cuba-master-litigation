#### C. Balance of Equities Strongly Favors Plaintiffs

Preserving blocked funds in interest-bearing escrow imposes zero administrative harm on the Government, whereas dissipation permanently destroys the Class's remedies.

#### D. The Public Interest Favors Injunction

Enforcing fiduciary obligations and preventing illegal exactions serves the highest public interest in the rule of law.
