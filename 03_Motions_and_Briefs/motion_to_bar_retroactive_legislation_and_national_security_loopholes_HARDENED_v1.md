### I. DUE PROCESS & SEPARATION OF POWERS BARRIERS

#### A. The Landgraf Anti-Retroactivity Principle

Under Landgraf v. USI Film Products, 511 U.S. 244 (1994), retroactive legislation impairing vested rights or imposing new liabilities is fundamentally disfavored. Congress cannot enact ad hoc retroactive statutes stripping accrued Tucker Act claims.

#### B. The United States v. Klein Separation of Powers Doctrine

Under United States v. Klein, 80 U.S. (13 Wall.) 128 (1871) and Bank Markazi v. Peterson, 578 U.S. 212 (2016), Congress violates Article III when it prescribes a rule of decision compelling the judiciary to dismiss a pending action against the United States.

### II. INAPPLICABILITY OF NATIONAL SECURITY DISCOVERY BLANKETS

#### A. Federal Rules of Civil Procedure Apply to Sovereign Litigants

When the United States enters federal court as a civil litigant, it is subject to the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure. It cannot claim blanket exemptions from Rule 34 production.
