### I. PRECLUSION OF STATE SECRETS AS SHIELD AND SWORD

#### A. The United States v. Reynolds Doctrine

Under United States v. Reynolds, 345 U.S. 1 (1953), the state secrets privilege is a narrow evidentiary privilege that must be scrutinized with care. The Government cannot invoke state secrets to suppress historical documents over a century old, nor can it use the privilege as both a sword and a shield.

#### B. Ancient Documents Exception (Fed. R. Evid. 803(16))

Historical records predating January 1, 1998, are admissible as ancient documents. Unsworn executive affidavits claiming national security cannot bar ancient public records.
