# DRAFT RESOLUTION OF THE UNITED NATIONS GENERAL ASSEMBLY
**Session:** Eighty-First Regular Session  
**Agenda Item:** Elimination of Unilateral Coercive Measures as an Instrument of Political and Economic Coercion  
**Document Code:** `A/81/L.___`  
**Sponsoring Delegations:** Republic of South Africa, Barbados, Republic of Namibia, Saint Vincent and the Grenadines, Republic of Ghana, et al.

## TEXT OF THE DRAFT RESOLUTION

*The General Assembly,*

*Guided by* the purposes and principles of the Charter of the United Nations, in particular the sovereign equality of States, the peaceful settlement of disputes, and the prohibition of the threat or use of force in international relations,

*Recalling* its landmark resolutions 1514 (XV) of 14 December 1960 containing the Declaration on the Granting of Independence to Colonial Countries and Peoples, and 1803 (XVII) of 14 December 1962 on Permanent Sovereignty over Natural Resources,

*Reaffirming* its annual resolutions concerning the 'Necessity of ending the economic, commercial and financial embargo imposed by the United States of America against Cuba', adopted consecutively for over three decades by overwhelming majorities of Member States,

*Emphasizing* the Articles on Responsibility of States for Internationally Wrongful Acts, adopted by the International Law Commission, particularly Article 14 regarding breaches having a continuing character and Article 31 establishing the obligation of full reparation,

*Considering* the Advisory Opinion of the International Court of Justice of 21 June 1971 in the Namibia proceedings, and the Advisory Opinion of 25 February 2019 in the Chagos Archipelago proceedings,

1. *Decides*, in accordance with Article 96, paragraph 1, of the Charter of the United Nations, to request the International Court of Justice, pursuant to Article 65 of the Statute of the Court, to render an Advisory Opinion on the following questions:

> **(a)** What are the legal obligations under the Charter of the United Nations, customary international law, and the law of treaties that apply to the unilateral economic, commercial, and financial measures imposed and maintained by the United States of America against Cuba and affecting third-party States and their nationals?
>
> **(b)** Does the continuous administrative blocking of private property, bank clearing accounts, and commercial revenues under unilateral regulations (31 C.F.R. Part 515) constitute an internationally wrongful act having a continuing character under international law, precluding the application of municipal statutes of limitations?
>
> **(c)** What are the legal consequences under international law, including the obligations of restitution, compensation, and guarantees of non-repetition, for the United States arising from military occupation land-title modifications and distressed auctions conducted between 1898 and 1902 in violation of the Hague Regulations and customary law of belligerent occupation?

2. *Invites* the Secretary-General to transmit the present resolution to the International Court of Justice, accompanied by all relevant documents and authenticated primary-source archives pursuant to Article 65, paragraph 2, of the Statute of the Court.
