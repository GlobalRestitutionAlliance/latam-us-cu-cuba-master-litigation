WHEREAS, Assignors desire to assign, and Assignee desires to accept, a five percent (5.00%) contingency beneficial interest in all restitution recoveries in consideration for Assignee deploying its sovereign standing, international treaty access, and diplomatic protection to prosecute arbitration claims against the corporate successors in neutral global arbitral seats.

## OPERATIVE PROVISIONS & LEGAL CONVENANTS

**CLAUSE 1: ASSIGNMENT OF FIVE PERCENT (5.00%) CONTINGENCY INTEREST**

Assignors hereby irrevocably grant, assign, transfer, and convey to Assignee an absolute, indefeasible five percent (5.00%) contingency beneficial interest in all gross proceeds, damages awards, cash settlements, restitution distributions, and monetized asset seizures resulting from the prosecution of claims against the U.S. corporate successors and their global subsidiaries.

**CLAUSE 2: COVENANT OF SOVEREIGN ARBITRATION PROSECUTION**

Assignee covenants and agrees to immediately commence, fully fund, and vigorously prosecute private international arbitrations against each of the targeted corporate successors pursuant to the UNCITRAL Arbitration Rules (2013/2021). Arbitrations shall be seated exclusively in neutral international jurisdictions, specifically Singapore (SIAC), Geneva (Swiss Arbitration Centre), and Dubai (DIAC).

**CLAUSE 3: APPLICATION OF THE 1958 NEW YORK CONVENTION**

The Parties explicitly agree that all arbitral awards rendered in proceedings commenced under this Agreement shall constitute binding commercial arbitral awards governed by the 1958 United Nations Convention on the Recognition and Enforcement of Foreign Arbitral Awards ('New York Convention'). Assignee covenants to enforce such awards across its global sovereign treaty network, attaching corporate assets under Articles III and IV of the Convention.

**CLAUSE 4: EXTENSION OF DIPLOMATIC PROTECTION & BIT STANDING**

Assignee, through its sovereign sponsor states, formally extends sovereign diplomatic protection to the assigned claims pursuant to customary international law (ILC Articles on Diplomatic Protection). Assignee shall invoke the dispute resolution and expropriation clauses of applicable Bilateral Investment Treaties (BITs) to prevent host states from sheltering defendant assets under the guise of municipal sovereign immunity.

**CLAUSE 5: ESTABLISHMENT OF IRREVOCABLE MULTILATERAL ESCROW ACCOUNT**

All funds, damages, settlement amounts, and proceeds recovered through arbitration or enforcement shall be paid directly into an Irrevocable Multilateral Sovereign Escrow Account established at a premier non-aligned financial institution in Zurich, Switzerland, or the Republic of Singapore. Within five (5) business days of receipt of cleared funds:

**  (a) Ninety-Five Percent (95.00%) shall be immediately disbursed to the Beneficial Proprietors Sovereign Restitution Trust; and
  (b) Five Percent (5.00%) shall be disbursed to Assignee as its earned sovereign contingency recovery fee.**

**CLAUSE 6: IRREVOCABILITY AND WAIVER OF SOVEREIGN DEFENSES**

Assignee warrants that it acts in a purely commercial capacity (*jure gestionis*) regarding this assignment and covenants never to assert sovereign immunity against Assignors. This Agreement is irrevocable and binding upon all successors, assigns, and state apparatuses.

**CLAUSE 7: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION**

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive law of Switzerland and the UNIDROIT Principles of International Commercial Contracts. Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration administered by the Swiss Arbitration Centre in Geneva under the Swiss Rules of International Arbitration.
