## V. CAUSES OF ACTION & LEGAL THEORIES

**COUNT 1: EXPROPRIATION WITHOUT PROMPT, ADEQUATE, AND EFFECTIVE COMPENSATION**

Respondents' acquisition, retention, and ongoing commercial exploitation of the latifundia without compensation to the beneficial proprietors violates customary international law and the standard articulated by the Permanent Court of International Justice in the *Factory at Chorzów* case (PCIJ Ser. A, No. 17 (1928)), requiring full restitution in kind or the payment of equivalent value.

**COUNT 2: BREACH OF INTERNATIONAL PUBLIC POLICY (ORDRE PUBLIC TRANSNATIONAL)**

Trafficking in uncompensated property derived from military occupation and colonial dispossession violates international public policy. No commercial entity may retain profits derived from an internationally wrongful act.

**COUNT 3: COMMERCIAL CONVERSION & FRAUDULENT CONVEYANCE**

The transfer of Cuban land assets into corporate shells and modern holding companies constitutes an intentional, continuous commercial conversion of Claimant's property under the common law of international commerce.

**COUNT 4: IMPOSITION OF CONSTRUCTIVE TRUST & DISGORGEMENT OF UNJUST ENRICHMENT**

Under equitable principles recognized globally, Respondents hold all assets, profits, and equity derived from the latifundia as constructive trustees for the benefit of the true beneficial owners.
