### DEFINITIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

1. **"Continuum"** refers to the 221-year historical, economic, and legal chain of U.S. executive, legislative, and corporate actions spanning from the 1805 Jeffersonian encroachment through modern 2026 sanctions jurisprudence (Vectors 1 through 1805).

2. **"Subject Property"** refers to the agricultural latifundia, sugar centrals, banking assets, and real estate holdings cataloged across the master evidentiary index.

### PART I: REQUESTS FOR ADMISSION (FED. R. CIV. P. 36)

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 1:** Admit that the United States Government exercised direct military government and customs receivership administration over Cuba during the periods 1899–1902 and 1906–1909.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 2:** Admit that the Platt Amendment imposed constitutional subordination upon the Republic of Cuba as a condition precedent to the withdrawal of U.S. military forces.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 3:** Admit that Executive Order 8389 and the Foreign Assets Control Regulations (FACR) instituted a comprehensive, ongoing freeze of Cuban sovereign and corporate assets that remains actively enforced in 2026.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 4:** Admit that the Department of State drafted and promulgated internal executive memoranda in 1960 (including the Mallory Memorandum) explicitly aimed at provoking internal economic distress and preventing resource access in Cuba.

**REQUEST FOR ADMISSION NO. 5:** Admit that major Wall Street banking syndicates, including National City Bank and J.P. Morgan Chase, held substantial mortgage liens and debt indentures on Cuban sugar latifundia prior to 1959.

### PART II: INTERROGATORIES (FED. R. CIV. P. 33)

**INTERROGATORY NO. 1:** Identify all executive orders, presidential proclamations, and administrative regulations from 1959 to the present day that authorize or enforce restrictions on sugar import quotas, commercial transactions, and asset freezes concerning Cuba.

**INTERROGATORY NO. 2:** Describe with specificity the interagency mechanisms, Department of the Treasury OFAC guidelines, and State Department protocols governing the administration and potential execution of Helms-Burton Title III claims and Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) certified claim multipliers.

**INTERROGATORY NO. 3:** State all factual and legal bases for Defendants' position that the 6-year statute of limitations under 28 U.S.C. § 2501 bars claims arising from property nationalizations, notwithstanding the continuous maintenance of economic sanctions, asset freezes, and Title III enforcement mechanisms through 2026.
