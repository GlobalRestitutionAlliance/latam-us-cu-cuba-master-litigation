### DEFINITIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

1. **"Continuum"** refers to the unbroken 221-year historical, economic, and regulatory sequence spanning 1805 through 2026 as codified across the 1,805 evidentiary vectors.
2. **"Document"** is defined in its broadest sense under Fed. R. Civ. P. 34(a)(1)(A), including all writings, drawings, graphs, charts, photographs, sound recordings, images, cables, telegrams, notarial ledgers, banking dockets, declassified memoranda, and ESI.
3. **"Subject Property"** refers to the sugar latifundia, agrarian centrals, infrastructure utilities, port facilities, and related real and intangible assets referenced in the Master Complaint.
4. **"Government Custodians"** includes all current and archived holdings maintained by the Department of State, Department of the Treasury (including OFAC), Department of Defense, National Archives and Records Administration (NARA Record Groups 59, 84, 226, 234, 263, 319, and 335), and Central Intelligence Agency (CIA CREST).

### REQUESTS FOR PRODUCTION

#### CATEGORY I: THE 1805–1898 ANTEBELLUM, SLAVE-TRADE & DIPLOMATIC PAPERS (VECTORS 1–250, 1001–1250, 1301–1400)

* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 1:** All unredacted diplomatic instructions, memoranda, and internal Cabinet minutes from 1805 through 1823 authored by Thomas Jefferson, James Madison, Albert Gallatin, or John Quincy Adams concerning the strategic containment, geographic absorption, or annexation of Cuba (Vectors 1201–1220).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 2:** All Treasury Department and Customs Service records from 1805 through 1860 documenting vessel manifests, customs clearances, and capital transfers between Southern ports (Charleston, Savannah, Mobile, New Orleans) and Cuban ports (Havana, Matanzas, Cienfuegos) involving slave-trade syndicates or plantation investments (Vectors 1051–1100).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 3:** All records and correspondence of U.S. Consul Nicholas Trist in Havana (1833–1841) relating to the fraudulent issuance of American maritime flags and documentation to vessels engaged in the transatlantic or Antillean slave trade (Vectors 1101–1120).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 4:** All State Department, Department of Justice, and Federal District Court dockets from 1850 through 1860 relating to the prosecution, investigation, or official tolerance of Caribbean filibustering expeditions organized by Narciso López, John A. Quitman, and the Knights of the Golden Circle (Vectors 1301–1350).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 5:** All consular dispatches, notarial copies, and intelligence reports maintained by the U.S. Consulate in Havana from 1861 through 1868 tracking Confederate commercial agents (including Charles J. Helm), cotton-for-munitions barter transactions, and refugee Southern planters acquiring sugar estates in western Cuba (Vectors 1351–1400).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 6:** The complete records, claim dockets, arbitral awards, and indemnity payout ledgers of the Spanish-American Claims Commission established pursuant to the Act of 1871 (17 Stat. 577) (Vectors 1407–1412, 1761–1765).

#### CATEGORY II: THE PROTECTORATE, WALL STREET RECEIVERSHIPS & PLATT AMENDMENT FILES (VECTORS 251–500, 1401–1500, 1741–1760)

* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 7:** All executive decrees, military orders, and administrative records generated by the U.S. Military Governors of Cuba (General John R. Brooke and General Leonard Wood) from 1899 to 1902 concerning the adjudication, restructuring, and validation of real estate titles and corporate concessions under Article VIII of the 1898 Treaty of Paris (Vectors 1434–1436).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 8:** All State Department and War Department records relating to the drafting, legislative imposition, and parliamentary enforcement of the Platt Amendment in 1901 (Vectors 1436, 1476, 1785).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 9:** All correspondence, audit reports, and concession agreements between the U.S. Provisional Government of Cuba (under Charles E. Magoon, 1906–1909) and American financial entities, including Speyer & Co., National City Bank of New York, and the United Fruit Company (Vectors 1441, 1456–1458).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 10:** All State Department and Treasury Department documentation regarding the 1904 Speyer & Co. loan indentures and subsequent financial agreements explicitly hypothecating or pledging Cuban national customs house receipts to foreign creditor syndicates (Vectors 1439, 1453).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 11:** All military intelligence files, diplomatic notes, and Marine Corps deployment logs concerning the 1912 armed intervention in Oriente Province to protect American sugar latifundia and infrastructure assets (Vectors 1461, 1481, 1739).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 12:** The complete mission reports, financial directives, and ministry reorganization mandates issued by General Enoch Crowder to the Cuban Ministry of Finance from 1919 through 1923 (Vectors 1447, 1468).

#### CATEGORY III: 1930s DIPLOMATIC INTERVENTION & PRE-1959 CARTEL FILES (VECTORS 1475–1550)

* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 13:** All declassified State Department dispatches, Ambassadorial diaries, and confidential cables authored by Ambassador Sumner Welles from May 1933 through December 1933 regarding the mediation of the Machado regime and the subsequent destabilization of the Ramón Grau San Martín government (Vectors 1448, 1484, 1486).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 14:** All records regarding the negotiation, quota schedules, and corporate lobbying surrounding the 1934 Reciprocal Trade Agreement Act and its impact on the Cuban sugar refining industry (Vectors 1487, 1511).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 15:** All intelligence reports and consular evaluations from 1952 through 1958 regarding U.S. corporate concessions, hotel/casino syndications, and military assistance agreements executed with the Fulgencio Batista administration (Vectors 1492–1495, 1508).

#### CATEGORY IV: THE 1959–1962 ECONOMIC WARFARE DIRECTIVES & DECLASSIFIED MEMORANDA (VECTORS 1501–1600, 1781–1800)

* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 16:** The original, unredacted Department of State Memorandum authored by Deputy Assistant Secretary Lester D. Mallory dated April 6, 1960, including all preparatory drafts, distribution lists, and interagency reaction papers (Vector 1516).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 17:** All Cabinet minutes, National Security Council (NSC) meeting transcripts, and interagency economic defense files from June through October 1960 relating to the drafting and issuance of Presidential Proclamation 3355 (reducing the Cuban sugar quota to zero) (Vectors 1553, 1789).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 18:** All declassified CIA operational records, Special Group (5412/2) meeting minutes, and Project AMTRUNK files from 1960 through 1965 detailing economic sabotage, agricultural destruction, and refinery interdiction directives (Vectors 1506, 1526, 1554, 1571).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 19:** All Department of the Treasury internal directives, legal opinions, and enforcement guidelines promulgated under Executive Order 8389 and the Trading with the Enemy Act (50 U.S.C. App. § 5(b)) establishing the Cuban Assets Control Regulations (31 C.F.R. Part 515) in February 1962 (Vectors 1555, 1787, 1791).

#### CATEGORY V: CLAIMS VALUATION, OFAC DOCKETS & MODERN HELMS-BURTON ENFORCEMENT (VECTORS 751–800, 1601–1700, 1761–1805)

* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 20:** The complete master dockets, certified decision files, valuation working papers, and individual claim ledgers compiled by the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) under the Cuban Claims Act of 1964 (Title V, 22 U.S.C. § 1643 et seq.), specifically including the certified records of the $1.8 billion master ledger (Vectors 1521, 1615, 1771–1775).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 21:** All Treasury Department (OFAC) dockets, settlement logs, and penalty assessments from 1962 to the present day identifying all blocked, frozen, or intercepted Cuban state, commercial, and private assets held within U.S. financial institutions (Vectors 1520, 1570, 1605, 1626).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 22:** All State Department Legal Adviser memoranda, interagency briefing books, and Title IV visa sanction dockets generated from 1996 through 2026 regarding the enforcement, administration, or suspension of Title III and Title IV of the Cuban Liberty and Democratic Solidarity (LIBERTAD / Helms-Burton) Act (Vectors 1595–1596, 1608, 1619, 1795).
* **REQUEST FOR PRODUCTION NO. 23:** All interagency reports, intelligence assessments, and determinations generated from 1982 through 2026 relating to the designation, rescission, and re-designation of Cuba as a State Sponsor of Terrorism under 50 U.S.C. § 4813 and 28 U.S.C. § 1605A (Vectors 1582, 1793, 1799).
