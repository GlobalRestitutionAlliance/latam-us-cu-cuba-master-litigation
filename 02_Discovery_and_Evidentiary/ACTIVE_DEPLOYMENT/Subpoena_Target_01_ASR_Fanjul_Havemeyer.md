## II. HISTORICAL CORPORATE SCOPE & VECTOR MAPPING

The Havemeyer Sugar Trust (American Sugar Refining Company) and the Cuban-American Sugar Company represented the supreme agro-industrial latifundia of Cuba, acquiring over 520,000 acres across Oriente and Matanzas provinces following the 1898 U.S. military intervention. Under General Manager Mario G. Menocal (later President of Cuba) and President Robert B. Hawley, these syndicates established Central Chaparra, Central Delicias, Central Tinguaro, Central Unidad, and the Puerto Padre deepwater shipping terminal. Certified by the FCSC in Claims CU-0044 and CU-0045 at over $81,057,000.00, these uncompensated landholdings form the direct historical lineage of modern ASR Group and Florida Crystals.

## III. TARGETED SCHEDULE OF DOCUMENT DEMANDS DUCES TECUM

### DEMAND 1: HAVEMEYER TRUST CARTEL RECORDS & TARIFF MANIPULATION

Produce all minutes, ledgers, and agreements of the American Sugar Refining Company and Havemeyer Sugar Trust (1891–1906) relating to the manipulation of the 1894 Wilson-Gorman Tariff, the orchestration of the 1903 Reciprocity Treaty, and the monopolistic allocation of raw sugar quotas between Cuban estates and domestic East Coast refineries.

### DEMAND 2: CENTRAL CHAPARRA & DELICIAS TITLE MUNIMENTS & MILITARY ORDERS

Produce all land grants, deeds of purchase, municipal auction adjudications, and eviction dockets (1898–1912) for Central Chaparra, Central Delicias, Central Tinguaro, and Central Unidad, including all titles altered pursuant to Military Orders issued under General Leonard Wood (NARA RG 140).

### DEMAND 3: PUERTO PADRE DEEPWATER TERMINAL & RAIL CONCESSIONS

Produce all concession decrees, franchise agreements, rights-of-way, and port operation charters for the 350 miles of private railway and the deepwater sugar-export terminal at Puerto Padre, Oriente Province.

CU-0044 and CU-0045, together with all IRS Form 1120 returns showing loss deductions claimed under 26 U.S.C. § 165(g)/(i) for Cuban expropriation.

### DEMAND 5: MODERN SUCCESSION, OFFSHORE ENTITIES & SUPPLY CHAIN COMMERCE
