## II. HISTORICAL CORPORATE SCOPE & VECTOR MAPPING

The United Fruit Company, operating through its subsidiary the Nipes Bay Company, acquired more than 325,000 acres of prime agrarian and coastal land in Oriente Province during the 1898–1902 U.S. military occupation. United Fruit constructed two of the largest agro-industrial sugar and transport empires in the world: Central Boston in Banes and Central Preston on Nipe Bay. These complexes included 200+ miles of private railways, extensive company towns, deepwater port docks at Puerto Banes and Preston, and massive private security forces. Certified in FCSC Claim Nos. CU-0021 and CU-2560 at $85,110,147.00, these assets form the core lineage of modern Chiquita Brands International and the Cutrale-Safra Group.
