## II. HISTORICAL CORPORATE SCOPE & VECTOR MAPPING

Chase National Bank and J.P. Morgan & Co. acted as the primary sovereign debt underwriters and fiscal agents for the Republic of Cuba. Between 1927 and 1930, Chase National Bank organized and managed the notorious $80,000,000 Public Works Loan Syndicate financing the regime of dictator Gerardo Machado, extracting exorbitant financing fees, underwriting discounts, and direct debt-servicing liens on Cuban customs revenues. Chase also financed the monopolistic expansion of the Electric Bond and Share Company (EBASCO) and the Cuban Cane Sugar Corporation.
