1. **Native Format Production:** All electronic files, databases, accounting ledgers, and spreadsheets must be produced in their native format (.XLSX, .CSV, .SQL, .PST, .EML, .MDF) with all original file metadata intact, including creation dates, author metadata, modification stamps, and file hash digests.

### IV. PRIVILEGE LOG REQUIREMENTS & PROHIBITION OF GENERALIZED PRIVILEGE CLAIMS
Pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 45(e)(2)(A), any person withholding subpoenaed information under a claim that it is privileged or subject to protection as trial-preparation material must:
1. Expressly make the claim; and
2. Describe the nature of the withheld documents, communications, or tangible things in a manner that, without revealing information itself privileged or protected, will enable the parties to assess the claim.

**Mandatory Log Form:** Any claim of privilege must be accompanied by an itemized, document-by-document Privilege Log served within thirty (30) days of subpoena service, setting forth: (a) Document ID / Bates Range; (b) Date of creation; (c) Author(s); (d) Addressee(s); (e) Blind copy recipient(s); (f) Subject matter; (g) Specific privilege asserted (attorney-client, work-product); and (h) Factual justification establishing that privilege has not been waived through disclosure to third parties, governmental agencies, or historical public SEC/FCSC filings.

R. Civ. P. 26(b)(3) or the attorney-client privilege.
