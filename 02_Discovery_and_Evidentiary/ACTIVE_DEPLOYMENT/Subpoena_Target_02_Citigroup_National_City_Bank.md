## II. HISTORICAL CORPORATE SCOPE & VECTOR MAPPING

Originating with Moses Taylor & Co.'s 19th-century sugar-slave merchant banking syndicate, The National City Bank of New York became the dominant financial hegemon of republican Cuba. During the 1920–1921 'Dance of the Millions' collapse, National City Bank engineered a massive contraction of agricultural credit, calling loans and foreclosing on over 50 major Cuban sugar mills. Through its wholly-owned subsidiary, the General Sugar Company, National City Bank operated over 600,000 acres of confiscated latifundia, consolidating Wall Street equity control over the Cuban agrarian economy.
