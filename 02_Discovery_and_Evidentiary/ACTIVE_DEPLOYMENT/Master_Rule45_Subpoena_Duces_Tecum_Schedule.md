## III. DEFINITIONS AND INSTRUCTIONS

A. 'Document' and 'ESI': The terms 'document' and 'electronically stored information' (ESI) are used in the broadest sense permitted by Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 34(a) and 45, encompassing every writing, recording, ledger, map, deed, charter, indenture, contract, telegram, cable, cablegram, memorandum, board minute, financial statement, tax filing, audio recording, microfilm, digital database, and email, whether draft or final.

B. 'Predecessor-in-Interest': Refers to any corporation, partnership, trust, bank, joint-stock association, or syndicate whose assets, stock, debts, liabilities, or business lines were directly or indirectly acquired, merged, consolidated, reorganized, or absorbed by You.

C. 'Possession, Custody, or Control': Includes documents in your actual possession, as well as documents in the possession of your officers, directors, attorneys, accountants, agents, subsidiaries, affiliated entities, offshore parent companies, or third-party archival storage contractors (e.g., Iron Mountain).

D. 'Latifundia': Refers to large-scale agrarian landed estates, sugar plantations, cattle ranches, timber tracts, and mineral concessions accumulated in Cuba between 1880 and 1959.

E. 'Trafficking': Has the meaning set forth in 22 U.S.C. § 6023(13), including any commercial use, purchase, receipt, possession, control, management, or profit derived from confiscated property.

F. Duty of Diligent Search: You are required to conduct an exhaustive search of all active, inactive, and offsite archival records, including warehouse indexes, corporate historical collections, and executive papers.

## IV. OMNIBUS SCHEDULE OF DOCUMENT DEMANDS DUCES TECUM

**DEMAND NO. 1: HISTORICAL GENERAL LEDGERS & SLAVE/INDENTURED LABOR TRANSITION (1880–1902)**

Produce all historical general ledgers, journals, cash books, slave payroll registers, Chinese indentured labor contracts (patrocinios), property purchase ledgers, and colonato tenant-farmer accounts maintained between January 1, 1880, and May 20, 1902, relating to the acquisition, construction, clearing, and operation of Cuban sugar plantations, centrales, and port facilities.

**DEMAND NO. 2: 1898–1902 MILITARY GOVERNMENT CONCESSIONS & LEONARD WOOD DISPATCHES**

Produce all deeds, concession contracts, railway franchise decrees, municipal land-auction purchase dockets, and official correspondence with Military Governor General Leonard Wood, General John R. Brooke, or Senator Orville H. Platt between January 1, 1898, and May 20, 1902, relating to Military Orders altering land registry titles, extinguishing communal haciendas comuneras, or granting deepwater port access (NARA Record Groups 140 and 94).

**DEMAND NO. 3: 1903–1920 SUGAR TRUST EXPANSION & TARIFF MONOPOLY CONCESSIONS**

Produce all syndicate agreements, trust indentures, corporate charters, board minutes, and lobbying correspondence relating to the 1903 U.S.-Cuba Reciprocity Treaty, the 20% preferential tariff quota, and the consolidation of sugar refiners under the American Sugar Refining Company (Havemeyer Sugar Trust), Cuban-American Sugar Company, and United Fruit Company.

**DEMAND NO. 4: 1920–1921 'DANCE OF THE MILLIONS' CRASH & NATIONAL CITY BANK FORECLOSURES**

Produce all loan ledgers, crop-lien mortgages, margin-call notices, foreclosure decrees, receivership dockets, and minutes of the General Sugar Company, Cuban-Dominican Sugar Co., and The National City Bank of New York (1920–1925), documenting the artificial deflation of sugar credit, seizure of 50+ defaulting Cuban mills, and conversion of sovereign agrarian debt into New York corporate equity.

**DEMAND NO. 5: 1925–1933 GERARDO MACHADO PUBLIC WORKS LOAN SYNDICATES**

Produce all underwriting agreements, debenture ledgers, secret commission accounts, presidential bribery vouchers, and diplomatic cables concerning the 1927–1930 $80,000,000 Cuban Public Works Loan Syndicate managed by Chase National Bank, J.P. Morgan & Co., and Blair & Co., and the resulting confiscatory taxation enforced on Cuban taxpayers.

**DEMAND NO. 6: 1934–1958 BATISTA ERA QUOTAS, OFFSHORE REMITTANCES & UTILITY MONOPOLIES**

Produce all records, ledgers, and correspondence concerning the allocation of U.S. Sugar Act quotas, payments to officials of the Fulgencio Batista administration, capital remittances to New York and Swiss banks, and utility concession contracts held by Compañía Cubana de Electricidad (EBASCO) and Cuban Telephone Company (ITT) between 1934 and December 31, 1958.

**DEMAND NO. 7: 1959–1968 FCSC CERTIFIED CLAIM DOCKETS (NARA RG 234) & BALANCE SHEET VALUATIONS**

Produce all primary claim dockets, audit workpapers, historical asset depreciation schedules, and sworn valuations submitted to the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission (FCSC) under the Cuban Claims Act of 1964, including all internal corporate reconciliations between the depreciated book value reported to the IRS and the unadjusted replacement cost claimed before the FCSC.

**DEMAND NO. 8: 100% EXPROPRIATION TAX WRITE-OFFS (26 U.S.C. § 165(g)/(i)) & DOUBLE-RECOVERY LEDGERS**

Produce all federal and state corporate income tax returns (specifically IRS Form 1120, Schedules D and M), Treasury closing agreements, and audit files from tax years 1960 through 1975, reflecting ordinary loss or capital loss deductions claimed under 26 U.S.C. § 165(g) and § 165(i) for uncompensated expropriation losses in Cuba, demonstrating that the federal government compensated You in full through 100% tax write-offs.

**DEMAND NO. 9: CORPORATE SUCCESSION, M&A DEEDS, SPINOFFS & ARCHIVAL REPOSITORY INDEXES**

Produce all certificates of merger, bills of sale, reorganization agreements, spinoff prospectuses, and asset-transfer schedules tracing the chain of title from each historical predecessor entity to your modern corporate structure, together with master indexes of all Iron Mountain and offsite archival depository boxes containing historical records.

**DEMAND NO. 10: MODERN OFFSHORE TAX HAVEN VEHICLES & HOLDING ENTITIES**

Produce organizational charts, register of directors, articles of incorporation, and annual accounting audits for all subsidiary companies, special purpose vehicles (SPVs), and trusts incorporated in the Cayman Islands, Bermuda, Panama, British Virgin Islands, Switzerland, Luxembourg, or Delaware that hold rights, trade lines, or proceeds derived from historical Cuban assets or successor operations.

**DEMAND NO. 11: HELMS-BURTON TITLE III TRAFFICKING ASSETS & COMMERCIAL LOGISTICS**

Produce all commercial contracts, shipping manifests, charter party agreements, pipeline usage logs, deepwater terminal leases, and revenue statements from 1996 to the present, reflecting the commercial trafficking (22 U.S.C. § 6082) in, or derivative economic benefit extracted from, land, deepwater ports (including Puerto Padre, Banes, Nipe Bay, Matanzas, Cárdenas, Havana Harbor), railway rights-of-way, and mineral deposits in Cuba.

**DEMAND NO. 12: ESI LITIGATION HOLD AUDIT LOGS & SPOLIATION CERTIFICATIONS**

Produce all written litigation hold notices, preservation instructions, suspension directives for automatic document purges, and IT audit logs demonstrating complete compliance with your affirmative duty to preserve ESI and physical records relevant to this litigation pursuant to Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(e).
