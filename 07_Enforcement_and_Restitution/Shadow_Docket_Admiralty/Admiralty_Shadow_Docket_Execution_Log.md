### IV. THE P&I CLUB LETTER OF UNDERTAKING (LOU) CASH-ESCROW CONVERSION
The primary economic goal of the Admiralty Shadow Docket is not the protracted physical detention of merchant ships, but the **immediate conversion of maritime arrests into irrevocable, liquid cash-escrow funds**.

#### Operational LOU Requirements:
1. **Club Standing:** Letters of Undertaking must be issued exclusively by an A-rated member of the **International Group of P&I Clubs** (e.g., Steamship Mutual, UK P&I Club, Gard, Skuld, Britannia, West of England).
2. **Escrow Guarantee:** The LOU must contain an unconditional promise to pay final judgments or court-approved settlement awards entered by the U.S. District Court for the District of Columbia or competent international tribunals up to the full arrest security value (e.g., **US$ 85,000,000.00 to US$ 250,000,000.00 per vessel**).
3. **Waiver of Defenses:** The LOU must expressly waive sovereign immunity, forum non conveniens, and act of state defenses, and consent to the jurisdiction of the arresting maritime court for enforcement of the bond.
