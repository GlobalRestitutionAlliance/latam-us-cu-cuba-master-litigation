## 1. THE APPLICANTS AND JURISDICTION

1.1 The Applicants apply pursuant to Section 37(1) of the Senior Courts Act 1981, which grants the High Court power to grant an injunction in all cases in which it appears to the court to be just and convenient to do so.

1.2 The English Commercial Court is the global jurisdictional nexus for the insurance and reinsurance contracts covering the ocean-going fleets of the corporate successors to the Sugar Trust and Wall Street latifundia.

## 2. THE ASSETS TO BE FROZEN: REINSURANCE & P&I PROCEEDS

2.1 The Respondent underwriters (including Lloyd's Syndicates 2623, 0033, 0510, Swiss Re Corporate Solutions, and the International Group of P&I Clubs) hold substantial premium reserves, reinsurance claims receivables, and collateral pledged for the issuance of Letters of Undertaking (LOUs).

2.2 These insurance funds represent the direct financial lifeblood shielding the corporate successors from the consequences of their maritime conversions.

## 3. GOOD ARGUABLE CASE AND RISK OF DISSIPATION

3.1 Good Arguable Case: The Applicants have demonstrated a powerful, verified restitution claim founded upon immutable NARA archival records and FCSC certified claims.

3.2 Risk of Dissipation: The Defendants have established complex offshore corporate holding networks in Bermuda, the Cayman Islands, and Panama specifically designed to move liquid charter earnings and insurance proceeds beyond the reach of creditors.

## 4. ORDER SOUGHT

**4.1 A Worldwide Freezing Order restraining the Corporate Successors from removing from England and Wales, or disposing of, dealing with, or diminishing the value of any assets up to the value of US$ 500,000,000.00.**

**4.2 An Interim Third-Party Debt Order pursuant to CPR Part 72 attaching all sums payable by Lloyd's of London underwriters, Swiss Re, and P&I Clubs to the Defendants under marine hull, cargo, and protection policies.**

**4.3 An order compelling the immediate disclosure of all insurance and reinsurance policy schedules covering the vessels identified in the Master Admiralty Schedule.**
