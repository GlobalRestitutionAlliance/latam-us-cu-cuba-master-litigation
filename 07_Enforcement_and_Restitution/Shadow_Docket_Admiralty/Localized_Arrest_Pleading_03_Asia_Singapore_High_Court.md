## 1. STATUTORY JURISDICTION UNDER THE HIGH COURT (ADMIRALTY JURISDICTION) ACT

1.1 The admiralty jurisdiction of this Honourable Court is invoked pursuant to Section 3(1)(g) (any claim for loss of or damage to goods carried in a ship) and Section 3(1)(h) (any claim arising out of any agreement relating to the carriage of goods or the use or hire of a ship).

1.2 Pursuant to Section 4(4) of the Act, an action in rem may be brought against the ship in connection with which the claim arose, or any other ship beneficially owned as respects all the shares therein by the person who would be liable on the claim in an action in personam.

## 2. PARTICULARS OF CLAIM: THE BELOT REFINERY & ENERGY CONVERSION

2.1 The Claimants are beneficial titleholders of the Havana Belot Refinery complex, expropriated without compensation in June 1960 following the unlawful refusal by Standard Oil Company of New Jersey (now ExxonMobil Corporation) to process crude oil, as certified in FCSC Claim CU-0064 ($71.6M certified value).

2.2 The Defendant vessel, M/T SEARIVER BELOT, is owned and operated by SeaRiver Maritime LLC, a wholly-owned subsidiary of ExxonMobil Corporation, which has derived billions in profits from converted petroleum supply chains.

## 3. CAVEAT AGAINST RELEASE & SHERIFF'S EXECUTION PROTOCOL

3.1 The Claimants have filed simultaneously herewith an Affidavit Leading Warrant of Arrest complying strictly with Order 70, Rule 4 of the Rules of Court.

3.2 The Sheriff of the Supreme Court of Singapore is requested to execute the Warrant of Arrest immediately upon the vessel dropping anchor within the Port of Singapore Western Petroleum Anchorage or Jurong Island terminal.

## 4. RELIEF CLAIMED

**4.1 Judgment against the Defendant Vessel M/T SEARIVER BELOT in the sum of US$ 220,000,000.00 together with interest and costs.**

**4.2 The issuance of a Warrant of Arrest directed to the Sheriff commanding the arrest and detention of the Defendant Vessel.**

**4.3 That the vessel remain under arrest until security is posted in the form of an irrevocable Banker's Guarantee or P&I Club Letter of Undertaking approved by the Registrar.**
