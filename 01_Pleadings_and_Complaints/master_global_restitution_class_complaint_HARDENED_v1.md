### I. JURISDICTIONAL HOOKS & SOVEREIGN IMMUNITY NULLIFICATION

#### A. Tucker Act & Equitable Recoupment

This Court possesses jurisdiction pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1491, 28 U.S.C. § 1331, and the law of constructive trusts. Where the sovereign retains funds or lands derived from ultra vires exactions and continuous trustee self-dealing, equity demands disgorgement.

#### B. The Mitchell Fiduciary Standard & Constructive Trust

United States v. Mitchell, 463 U.S. 206 (1983) and United States v. White Mountain Apache Tribe, 537 U.S. 465 (2003) (Government assumption of pervasive fiduciary control creates an enforceable constructive trust waiving sovereign immunity). Under Beatty v. Guggenheim Exploration Co., 225 N.Y. 380 (1919) (Cardozo, J.), a constructive trust is the formula through which the conscience of equity finds expression when property has been acquired in such circumstances that the holder may not in good conscience retain the beneficial interest.

### II. FACTUAL BASIS FOR UNIVERSAL RESTITUTION ACROSS THE 221-YEAR CONTINUUM

#### A. The Wealth Extraction Ledger (1898–1958)

As documented in Gordon Pollock's historical study (Gordon Pollock, The American Occupation of Cuba, 1898–1902, HISTORIA, vol. 12, at 1-28 (2003).), U.S. capital systematically absorbed Cuba's primary wealth. General Leonard Wood's Military Government altered land registries (NARA RG 140) to drive smallholders into default, while General Wood privately declared to President McKinley that 'There is, of course, no independence left in Cuba after the Platt Amendment. With the control which we have over its foreign relations and its financial affairs, and with the naval stations which we hold, the island is practically an American dependency, and we practically control the sugar trade of the world. (Major General Leonard Wood, Military Governor of Cuba, Letter to President William McKinley, Oct. 29, 1901, Leonard Wood Papers, Library of Congress Manuscript Division, Container 31).'. Between 1902 and 1958, the American Sugar Refining Company, Chase National Bank, and National City Bank extracted over $8.4 billion in uncompensated agrarian yields, leaving the domestic populace structurally impoverished.

#### B. The Blocked Res & Active 2026 OFAC Enforcement

Since 1963, the Department of the Treasury (OFAC) has maintained frozen clearinghouse funds, bank accounts, and commercial balances under 31 C.F.R. Part 515. Defendants have used these frozen assets as diplomatic leverage, refusing to establish an independent escrow or restorative trust for the actual dispossessed victims.
