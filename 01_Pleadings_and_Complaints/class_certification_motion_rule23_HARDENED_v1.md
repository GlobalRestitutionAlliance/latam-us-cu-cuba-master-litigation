### I. PRELIMINARY STATEMENT & RELIEF REQUESTED

#### Certification of Master Class and Discrete Subclasses

Plaintiffs respectfully move this Court for an order certifying this action as a Class Action pursuant to Federal Rules of Civil Procedure 23(a), 23(b)(2), and 23(b)(3). The proposed Class comprises all individuals, estates, and hereditary successors whose properties, ancestral lands, wages, or bank accounts were expropriated, encumbered, or blocked by the United States Government and its military/administrative apparatus across the 221-year continuum (1805–2026).

### II. SATISFACTION OF RULE 23(a) PREREQUISITES

#### A. Numerosity (Rule 23(a)(1))

The Class includes tens of thousands of dispersed property owners, heirs, and Afro-Cuban descendants located across Florida, New York, Puerto Rico, Cuba, and Europe. Joinder is impracticable.

#### B. Commonality (Rule 23(a)(2))

Under Wal-Mart Stores, Inc. v. Dukes, 564 U.S. 338 (2011), commonality requires a common contention capable of class-wide resolution. Here, the central legal questions are: (1) Did the U.S. Military Government assume a fiduciary trust under Mitchell?; (2) Did General Leonard Wood's land decrees constitute an illegal taking?; (3) Does active 2026 OFAC enforcement reset the limitations clock under ARSIWA Art. 14? Resolution of these threshold questions determines liability for the entire Class.

#### C. Typicality (Rule 23(a)(3))

The claims of the Named Class Representatives arise from the identical course of federal conduct and rely on the identical legal theories under the Fifth Amendment, Mitchell, and Tucker Act.

#### D. Adequacy of Representation (Rule 23(a)(4))

Class Representatives have vigorously prosecuted this action without conflict of interest, represented by specialized Omni-Counsel Jurisprudential Warfare Command.

### II. SATISFACTION OF RULE 23(b)(2) & 23(b)(3)

#### A. Certification under Rule 23(b)(2) for Injunctive & Declaratory Relief

Defendants have acted and refused to act on grounds generally applicable to the Class, maintaining an active, uniform trade embargo and asset block under 31 C.F.R. Part 515. Declaratory relief establishing fiduciary breach and injunctive relief imposing a constructive trust are appropriate for the Class as a whole.

#### B. Certification under Rule 23(b)(3) for Restitution and Damages

Common questions of sovereign liability, historical state action, and statutory interpretation overwhelmingly predominate over individual damage calculations. Under Fed. R. Civ. P. 53, a Special Master can execute mechanical formulaic damage distributions following Phase I liability determination.

### IV. SUN TZU ENTRAPMENT: COMMONALITY CHECKMATE

#### Strategic Dilemma for the Department of Justice

Under Comcast Corp. v. Behrend, 569 U.S. 27 (2013), damages must measure only those damages attributable to that theory of liability.
