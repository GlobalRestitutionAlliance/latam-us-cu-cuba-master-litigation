# UNITED STATES COURT OF APPEALS FOR THE DISTRICT OF COLUMBIA CIRCUIT
**Case No.: 26-______**

IN RE ESTATE OF THE DISPOSSESSED CUBAN PROPRIETORS, ET AL.,  
*Petitioners*,  
v.  
NATIONAL ARCHIVES AND RECORDS ADMINISTRATION, ET AL.,  
*Respondents*.

## [PROPOSED] ORDER GRANTING WRIT OF MANDAMUS AND MANDATORY JUDICIAL NOTICE

Upon consideration of Petitioners' Emergency Petition for a Writ of Mandamus, the Motion for Mandatory Judicial Notice pursuant to Fed. R. Evid. 201, the supporting archival schedules, and any response filed by Respondents, it is hereby:

ORDERED that Petitioners' Motion for Mandatory Judicial Notice is GRANTED pursuant to Federal Rule of Evidence 201(c)(2). The Court takes judicial notice of the official promulgation, existence, and authentic contents of: (1) NARA Record Group 140 (Civil Orders Nos. 62, 89, 139); (2) NARA Record Group 234 (Foreign Claims Settlement Commission Title V Certified Claims Ledgers); (3) NARA Record Group 94 (General Leonard Wood's December 6, 1900 dispatch to Senator Orville H. Platt); and (4) Library of Congress Manuscript Division (Leonard Wood Papers, Container 31 dispatch to President McKinley); and it is further

ORDERED that a Writ of Mandamus shall issue directing Respondents National Archives and Records Administration, Dr. Colleen J. Shogan, and the Foreign Claims Settlement Commission to make available to Petitioners certified, unredacted copies of all records specified in Petitioners' Archival Schedule within thirty (30) days of entry of this Order; and it is further

ORDERED that Respondent Office of Foreign Assets Control (OFAC) shall file a Verified Statement of Compliance within twenty (20) days detailing whether and to what extent historical property muniments from NARA RG 140 and RG 234 are utilized in active 2026 asset blocking determinations under 31 C.F.R. Part 515.

SO ORDERED.
